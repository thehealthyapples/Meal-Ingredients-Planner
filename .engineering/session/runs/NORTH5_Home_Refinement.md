## Objective
The direction is **chosen**, not searched for. The **archway** is the canonical architectural
signature of Home; the **orchard-derived material language** is the correct material direction.
Do not redesign, do not return to dashboard thinking. **Refine the single vision** into **three
variations of the same Home** — Elegant Entrance Hall · Connected Living Space · The Family Hearth —
that all clearly belong to one design family. Discover the *definitive* arrival.
**Design exploration only. Nothing implemented. No CSS. No product change.**

## Outcome
Three refined proposals authored into `docs/implementation/NORTH5_HOME_REFINEMENT.md`, all built from
one shared **kit of parts** (the arch · the continuous plaster wall · the furniture principle · the
floor · the light · the material flow · the palette · the blossom threshold) so they are provably one
family. The refinement adds exactly one new rule — **wall → furniture → floor, never upper-band →
lower-band** — with a checkable test (*cover the furniture with your hand; is the wall continuous
behind where it was?*). Each variation given desktop / tablet / mobile **annotated elevations** (not
renders, by instruction — no CSS written), an emotional journey, architectural decisions, strengths
and weaknesses. Governance carried from NORTH4 (the arch is a drawn frame at E3 → needs the named
§ 6 amendment; the sun is upper-right vs § 7; serif vs Caveat) noted as the *path to build*, not
re-litigated — the direction is decided.
