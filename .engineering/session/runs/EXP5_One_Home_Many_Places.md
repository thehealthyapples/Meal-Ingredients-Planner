## Mission

Act as THA's experience designer and art director. Investigate how THA can feel
like **one warm, lived-in home with several distinct places, all connected to
the same orchard** — spatial psychology and art direction, not literal rooms or
skeuomorphism. Investigation only; no implementation.

Core principle: *"Every place in THA should feel different, but every window
should look onto the same orchard."*

Deliver in `docs/investigations/ux/EXP5_ONE_HOME_MANY_PLACES.md`:
1. Governing concept. 2. Domain-by-domain place & art-direction map (11
domains × 9 attributes). 3. Shared materials/light/depth vocabulary. 4. Orchard
exposure rules. 5. Living-details library with strict limits. 6. Existing UI
that supports the concept. 7. Areas that conflict. 8. Three recommended
dev-only prototype domains. 9. First prototype workstream recommendation.
