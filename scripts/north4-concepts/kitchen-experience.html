Slow Sunday roast

Serves 4Hands-on 25 minIn the oven 2 hrs
You’ve reached for this before — it has featured in your plans three times.
On the worktop
  • Free-range chicken1.6 kg
  • Maris Piper potatoes1 kg
  • Carrots & parsnips500 g
  • Fresh thyme & rosemarya few sprigs
  • Garlic1 bulb
  • Butter, lemon, sea saltto finish
The method, unhurried
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Sit the chicken in a roasting tin, rub with butter, and season well.
  2. Halve the potatoes and roots; tuck them around the bird with the garlic and herbs.
  3. Roast for two hours, basting once or twice, until the skin is deep gold and the juices run clear.
  4. Rest for fifteen minutes under foil. Finish with lemon and the pan juices, and bring it to the table.
