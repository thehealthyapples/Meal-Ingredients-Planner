## THA-451 — Indian-Style Lentil, Butternut Squash & Carrot Skillet
**Category:** dinner  
**Cuisine inspiration:** Indian-inspired  
**Serves:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 minutes  
**Cook:** 35 minutes  
**Difficulty:** medium

### Ingredients
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, crushed
- 1 x 400g tin lentils, drained
- 250g butternut squash, chopped
- 250g carrot, chopped
- 250g quinoa
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- Juice of 1 lemon or lime
- Fresh herbs, to serve
- Optional chilli flakes or sliced jalapeños, added after serving

### Method
1. Heat olive oil in a large pan and cook onion for 6–8 minutes.
2. Add garlic and spices and cook for 1 minute.
3. Add lentil, butternut squash and carrot, stirring to coat in the flavour base.
4. Add tomatoes, stock or water as listed and simmer until tender.
5. Cook or stir in the quinoa as appropriate for the dish.
6. Season lightly and serve with fresh herbs, with chilli or jalapeños added at the table.

### Why this works for THA
Lentil, butternut squash, carrot and quinoa create a filling plant-forward meal built from recognisable ingredients and simple seasoning.

### Planner readability notes
- category: dinner
- contains lentil
- contains butternut squash
- contains carrot
- contains quinoa
- mild base recipe

**Prep:** 10 minutes  
**Cook:** 20 minutes  
**Difficulty:** easy

### Ingredients
- 1 x 400g tin chickpeas, drained
- 120g brown rice
- 100g fennel, grated or finely chopped
- 2 tbsp mixed seeds
- 1 tsp ginger
- 1 tbsp olive oil or natural yoghurt
- Lemon or lime juice

### Method
1. Prepare the vegetables and place in a bowl.
2. Add the protein, grain or oats, seeds and seasoning.
3. Mix until spoonable or able to hold together.
4. Shape, chill or portion depending on the recipe form.
5. Serve as a whole-food snack or lunchbox extra.

### Why this works for THA
Chickpea, fennel, aubergine and brown rice create a filling plant-forward meal built from recognisable ingredients and simple seasoning.

## THA-500 — Georgian-Style Onion, Red Cabbage & Haricot Bean Fruit Pot
**Category:** snack  
**Cuisine inspiration:** Georgian-inspired  
**Serves:** 8  
**Prep:** 10 minutes  
**Cook:** 20 minutes  
**Difficulty:** easy

### Ingredients
- 1 x 400g tin haricot beans, drained
- 120g pearl barley
- 100g onion, grated or finely chopped
- 2 tbsp mixed seeds
- 1 tsp coriander
- 1 tbsp olive oil or natural yoghurt
- Lemon or lime juice

### Method
1. Prepare the vegetables and place in a bowl.
2. Add the protein, grain or oats, seeds and seasoning.
3. Mix until spoonable or able to hold together.
4. Shape, chill or portion depending on the recipe form.
5. Serve as a whole-food snack or lunchbox extra.

### Why this works for THA
Haricot Bean, onion, red cabbage and pearl barley create a filling plant-forward meal built from recognisable ingredients and simple seasoning.

- contains onion
- contains red cabbage
- contains pearl barley
- mild base recipe
