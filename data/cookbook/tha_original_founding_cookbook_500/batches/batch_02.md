# THA Original Founding Cookbook — Batch 02

## THA-051 — Pakistani-Style Feta, Parsnip & Fennel Egg Skillet
**Category:** breakfast  
**Cuisine inspiration:** Pakistani-inspired  
**Serves:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 minutes  
**Cook:** 10 minutes  
**Difficulty:** easy

### Ingredients
- 250g feta
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 150g parsnip, chopped
- 150g fennel, chopped
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 4 wholegrain wraps or slices of toast
- Black pepper

### Method
1. Heat olive oil in a pan.
2. Cook parsnip and fennel until softened.
3. Add the protein and warm through or cook until done.
4. Season lightly with the listed spice.
5. Serve with wholegrain toast or wraps.

### Why this works for THA
Feta, parsnip, fennel and wholegrain bread create a filling plant-forward meal built from recognisable ingredients and simple seasoning.

### Planner readability notes

- contains parsnip
- contains fennel
- contains wholegrain bread

- Juice of 1 lemon or lime
- Fresh herbs, to serve
- Optional chilli flakes or sliced jalapeños, added after serving

### Method
1. Heat olive oil in a large pan and cook onion for 6–8 minutes.
2. Add garlic and spices and cook for 1 minute.
3. Add cannellini bean, courgette and green bean, stirring to coat in the flavour base.
4. Add tomatoes, stock or water as listed and simmer until tender.
5. Cook or stir in the brown rice as appropriate for the dish.
6. Season lightly and serve with fresh herbs, with chilli or jalapeños added at the table.

### Why this works for THA
Cannellini Bean, courgette, green bean and brown rice create a filling plant-forward meal built from recognisable ingredients and simple seasoning.
