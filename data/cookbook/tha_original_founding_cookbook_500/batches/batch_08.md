## THA-351 — Indian-Style Chicken Thigh, Butternut Squash & Cabbage Skillet 6
**Category:** dinner  
**Cuisine inspiration:** Indian-inspired  
**Serves:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 minutes  
**Cook:** 35 minutes  
**Difficulty:** medium

### Ingredients
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, crushed
- 300g quality chicken thigh
- 250g butternut squash, chopped
- 250g cabbage, chopped
- 250g potato
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- Juice of 1 lemon or lime
- Fresh herbs, to serve
- Optional chilli flakes or sliced jalapeños, added after serving

### Method
1. Heat olive oil in a large pan and cook onion for 6–8 minutes.
2. Add garlic and spices and cook for 1 minute.
3. Add chicken thigh, butternut squash and cabbage, stirring to coat in the flavour base.
4. Add tomatoes, stock or water as listed and simmer until tender.
5. Cook or stir in the potato as appropriate for the dish.
6. Season lightly and serve with fresh herbs, with chilli or jalapeños added at the table.

### Why this works for THA
A modest amount of quality chicken thigh supports a plate led by butternut squash, cabbage and potato, keeping the meal plant-forward while still familiar.

### Planner readability notes
- category: dinner
- contains chicken thigh
- contains butternut squash
- contains cabbage
- contains potato
