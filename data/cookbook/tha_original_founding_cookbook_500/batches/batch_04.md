## THA-151 — Thai-Style Butter Bean, Cherry Tomato & Spinach Lunch Bowl
**Category:** lunch  
**Cuisine inspiration:** Thai-inspired  
**Serves:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 minutes  
**Cook:** 20 minutes  
**Difficulty:** easy

### Ingredients
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, crushed
- 1 x 400g tin butter beans, drained
- 250g cherry tomato, chopped
- 250g spinach, chopped
- 250g wholegrain wrap
- 1 tsp ginger
- 1 tsp lime
- 200ml reduced-fat coconut milk
- Juice of 1 lemon or lime
- Fresh herbs, to serve
- Optional chilli flakes or sliced jalapeños, added after serving

### Method
1. Heat olive oil in a large pan and cook onion for 6–8 minutes.
2. Add garlic and spices and cook for 1 minute.
3. Add butter bean, cherry tomato and spinach, stirring to coat in the flavour base.
4. Add tomatoes, stock or water as listed and simmer until tender.
5. Cook or stir in the wholegrain wrap as appropriate for the dish.
6. Season lightly and serve with fresh herbs, with chilli or jalapeños added at the table.

### Why this works for THA
Butter Bean, cherry tomato, spinach and wholegrain wrap create a filling plant-forward meal built from recognisable ingredients and simple seasoning.

### Planner readability notes

- contains butter bean
- contains cherry tomato
- contains spinach
- contains wholegrain wrap
