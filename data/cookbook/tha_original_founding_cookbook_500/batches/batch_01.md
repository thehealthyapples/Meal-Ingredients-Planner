## THA-001 — Apple, Oat & Cinnamon Morning Bowl
**Category:** breakfast  
**Cuisine inspiration:** British family kitchen  
**Serves:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 minutes  
**Cook:** 10 minutes  
**Difficulty:** easy

### Ingredients
- 160g rolled wholegrain oats
- 600ml semi-skimmed milk or unsweetened oat milk
- 200ml water
- 2 eating apples, 1 grated and 1 diced
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1 tbsp mixed seeds
- 1 tbsp chopped walnuts, optional
- 1 tsp honey per bowl, optional

### Method
1. Put the oats, milk, water, grated apple and cinnamon in a saucepan.
2. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook for 6–8 minutes, stirring often, until creamy.
3. Stir in half the diced apple for the final minute.
4. Spoon into bowls and top with remaining apple, seeds and walnuts.
5. Finish with a little honey only if wanted.

### Why this works for THA
Wholegrain oats, apple, seeds and nuts create a filling whole-food breakfast with sweetness from fruit rather than added sugar.

### Planner readability notes
- contains wholegrain oats
- contains fresh apple
- mild base recipe
- breakfast
- no-cook overnight option possible

## THA-002 — Chickpea, Lemon & Spinach Soup
**Category:** lunch  
**Cuisine inspiration:** Mediterranean-inspired  
**Serves:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 minutes  
**Cook:** 25 minutes  
**Difficulty:** easy

### Ingredients
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 carrots, diced
- 2 celery sticks, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, crushed
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 2 x 400g tins chickpeas, drained
- 1.2 litres low-salt vegetable stock
- 1 tbsp tomato purée
- 100g fresh spinach, chopped
- Juice of 1 lemon
- Black pepper and a little salt
- Wholegrain bread, to serve

### Method
1. Heat the olive oil in a large pan and cook onion, carrots and celery for 8 minutes.
2. Add garlic, cumin and coriander and cook for 1 minute.
3. Stir in tomato purée, chickpeas and stock.
4. Simmer for 12–15 minutes until the vegetables are tender.
5. Blitz a third of the soup if a thicker texture is wanted.
6. Stir in spinach and lemon juice, season lightly and serve with wholegrain bread.

### Why this works for THA
Chickpeas, vegetables and spinach make a filling plant-forward lunch with flavour from lemon, cumin and coriander rather than heavy salt.

### Planner readability notes
- contains chickpeas
- contains spinach
- mild base recipe
- lunch
- batch-cook friendly
- freezer friendly before adding lemon

## THA-003 — Basil Tomato Wholewheat Pasta
**Category:** dinner  
**Cuisine inspiration:** Italian-inspired  
**Serves:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 minutes  
**Cook:** 25 minutes  
**Difficulty:** easy

### Ingredients
- 350g wholewheat pasta
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, chopped
- 2 x 400g tins chopped tomatoes
- 1 tbsp tomato purée
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp balsamic vinegar
- Black pepper and a little salt
- 1 large handful fresh basil
- 30g Parmesan or vegetarian hard cheese, optional

### Method
1. Cook onion gently in olive oil for 8 minutes.
2. Add garlic and oregano and cook for 1 minute.
3. Stir in tomato purée, chopped tomatoes and balsamic vinegar.
4. Simmer for 15–18 minutes until thickened.
5. Cook pasta until just tender, reserving a little pasta water.
6. Toss pasta through the sauce and finish with basil.

### Why this works for THA
A familiar pasta meal made from real tomatoes, onion, garlic and herbs, with wholewheat pasta for extra fibre and fullness.

### Planner readability notes
- contains wholewheat pasta
- tomato sauce from scratch
- mild base recipe
- dinner
- sauce is freezer friendly

## THA-004 — Golden Potato, Chickpea & Spinach Curry
**Category:** dinner  
**Cuisine inspiration:** Indian-inspired  
**Serves:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 minutes  
**Cook:** 30 minutes  
**Difficulty:** easy

### Ingredients
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, crushed
- 20g fresh ginger, grated
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp ground turmeric
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 500g potatoes, cubed
- 1 x 400g tin chopped tomatoes
- 1 x 400g tin chickpeas, drained
- 300ml low-salt vegetable stock
- 100g fresh spinach
- Brown basmati rice or wholemeal flatbreads, to serve

### Method
1. Cook onion in oil for 8 minutes until soft.
2. Add garlic and ginger for 1 minute, then stir in spices.
3. Add potatoes, chopped tomatoes and stock.
4. Cover and simmer for 15 minutes.
5. Add chickpeas and cook for 8–10 minutes more until potatoes are tender.
6. Stir through spinach and serve with brown rice or wholemeal flatbreads.

### Why this works for THA
A mild curry made from aromatics and spices, with chickpeas, potatoes and spinach for a filling plant-forward dinner.

### Planner readability notes
- contains chickpeas
- contains potatoes
- contains spinach
- mild base recipe
- batch-cook friendly
- freezer friendly

## THA-005 — Gentle Taco Rice Bowls
**Category:** dinner  
**Cuisine inspiration:** Mexican-inspired  
**Serves:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 minutes  
**Cook:** 25 minutes  
**Difficulty:** easy

### Ingredients
- 250g brown rice
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 1 red pepper, diced
- 2 garlic cloves, crushed
- 250g quality lean beef mince
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tbsp tomato purée
- 1 x 400g tin chopped tomatoes
- 1 x 400g tin black beans, drained
- 150g sweetcorn
- Shredded lettuce, tomato, avocado, lime and natural yoghurt, to serve

### Method
1. Cook brown rice until tender.
2. Cook onion and pepper in olive oil for 6–7 minutes.
3. Add garlic and beef mince and brown well.
4. Stir in cumin, smoked paprika, oregano and tomato purée.
5. Add tomatoes, black beans and sweetcorn and simmer for 10 minutes.
6. Serve over rice with lettuce, tomato, avocado, lime and yoghurt.

### Why this works for THA
A small amount of quality beef is stretched with beans, vegetables and brown rice, keeping the meal plant-forward and filling.

### Planner readability notes
- contains beef mince
- contains black beans
- contains brown rice
- mild base recipe
- batch-cook friendly
- freezer friendly

## THA-006 — Roast Vegetable & Butter Bean Traybake
**Category:** dinner  
**Cuisine inspiration:** Mediterranean-inspired  
**Serves:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 minutes  
**Cook:** 40 minutes  
**Difficulty:** easy

### Ingredients
- 2 courgettes, cut into chunks
- 2 peppers, cut into chunks
- 1 red onion, cut into wedges
- 300g cherry tomatoes
- 2 x 400g tins butter beans, drained
- 3 garlic cloves, sliced
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp dried mixed herbs
- 1 tbsp tomato purée
- 1 tsp balsamic vinegar
- Fresh parsley or basil, chopped
- Wholegrain bread or brown rice, to serve

### Method
1. Heat oven to 200°C fan.
2. Toss courgettes, peppers, onion and tomatoes with garlic, olive oil and herbs.
3. Roast for 20 minutes.
4. Whisk tomato purée, balsamic vinegar and a little water into a dressing.
5. Add butter beans and dressing to tray and roast for 15–18 minutes more.
6. Scatter with herbs and serve with wholegrain bread or brown rice.

### Why this works for THA
Vegetables lead the tray, butter beans make it filling, and the sauce is built from tomato purée, vinegar, herbs and roasting juices.

### Planner readability notes
- contains butter beans
- one-tray method
- mild base recipe
- dinner
- batch-cook friendly

## THA-007 — Chicken, Leek & Wholewheat Orzo One-Pot
**Category:** dinner  
**Cuisine inspiration:** British-Mediterranean family kitchen  
**Serves:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 minutes  
**Cook:** 35 minutes  
**Difficulty:** medium

### Ingredients
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 4 higher-welfare skinless boneless chicken thighs, cut into pieces
- 2 leeks, sliced and washed
- 2 carrots, diced
- 2 garlic cloves, crushed
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 250g wholewheat orzo
- 900ml low-salt chicken stock
- 100g frozen peas
- 80g fresh spinach
- Zest and juice of 1 lemon
- Grated hard cheese, optional

### Method
1. Brown chicken pieces in olive oil, then lift out.
2. Cook leeks and carrots for 6–7 minutes.
3. Add garlic and thyme for 1 minute.
4. Return chicken to pan, add orzo and stock and simmer for 12–14 minutes, stirring often.
5. Stir in peas and spinach for 2–3 minutes.
6. Finish with lemon zest and juice.

### Why this works for THA
A modest amount of quality chicken supports a pot led by leeks, carrots, peas, spinach and wholewheat orzo.

### Planner readability notes
- contains chicken
- contains wholewheat orzo
- one-pot method
- mild base recipe
- best eaten fresh

## THA-008 — Lentil & Root Vegetable Cottage Pie
**Category:** dinner  
**Cuisine inspiration:** British family kitchen  
**Serves:** 6  
**Prep:** 25 minutes  
**Cook:** 45 minutes  
**Difficulty:** medium

### Ingredients
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 carrots, diced
- 2 celery sticks, diced
- 1 parsnip, diced
- 2 garlic cloves, crushed
- 1 tbsp tomato purée
- 300g dried green or brown lentils
- 1 x 400g tin chopped tomatoes
- 600ml low-salt vegetable stock
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 150g frozen peas
- 1kg potatoes, chopped
- 200g swede, chopped
- 30g butter and 3 tbsp milk for mash

### Method
1. Cook onion, carrot, celery and parsnip in olive oil for 10 minutes.
2. Add garlic and tomato purée.
3. Add lentils, chopped tomatoes, stock and thyme and simmer until thick and tender.
4. Stir in peas.
5. Boil potatoes and swede, then mash with butter and milk.
6. Spoon lentil base into a dish, top with mash and bake at 200°C for 25–30 minutes.

### Why this works for THA
Lentils and root vegetables make a filling family bake with savoury depth from slow-cooked vegetables rather than packet gravy.

### Planner readability notes
- contains lentils
- contains root vegetables
- batch-cook friendly
- freezer friendly
- mild base recipe

## THA-010 — Roasted Carrot, Chickpea & Herb Grain Salad
**Category:** side  
**Cuisine inspiration:** Middle Eastern-inspired  
**Serves:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 minutes  
**Cook:** 30 minutes  
**Difficulty:** easy

### Ingredients
- 150g wholegrain bulgur wheat
- 400g carrots, cut into batons
- 1 x 400g tin chickpeas, drained
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- Zest and juice of 1 lemon
- 3 spring onions, sliced
- A handful parsley and mint, chopped
- 2 tbsp mixed seeds

### Method
1. Heat oven to 200°C fan.
2. Toss carrots and chickpeas with olive oil, cumin and smoked paprika.
3. Roast for 25–28 minutes until tender.
4. Cook bulgur wheat according to packet and fluff with a fork.
5. Whisk lemon zest, lemon juice and a little olive oil.
6. Combine bulgur, carrots, chickpeas, spring onions and herbs, then scatter with seeds.

### Why this works for THA
Wholegrain bulgur, chickpeas, carrots, herbs and seeds make a sturdy whole-food side or lunchbox main.

### Planner readability notes
- contains chickpeas
- contains wholegrain bulgur wheat
- contains carrots
- lunchbox friendly
- can be served warm or cold

**Cuisine inspiration:** Pakistani-inspired  
**Serves:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 minutes  
**Cook:** 10 minutes  
**Difficulty:** easy

### Ingredients
- 250g feta
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 150g tomato, chopped
- 150g cauliflower, chopped
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 4 wholegrain wraps or slices of toast
- Black pepper

### Method
1. Heat olive oil in a pan.
2. Cook tomato and cauliflower until softened.
3. Add the protein and warm through or cook until done.
4. Season lightly with the listed spice.
5. Serve with wholegrain toast or wraps.
