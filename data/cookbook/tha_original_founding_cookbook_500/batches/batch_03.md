## THA-101 — Thai-Style Tuna, Cherry Tomato & Pumpkin Jacket Potato
**Category:** lunch  
**Cuisine inspiration:** Thai-inspired  
**Serves:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 minutes  
**Cook:** 20 minutes  
**Difficulty:** medium

### Ingredients
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, crushed
- 4 quality tuna fillets or 350g tuna
- 250g cherry tomato, chopped
- 250g pumpkin, chopped
- 250g pearl barley
- 1 tsp ginger
- 1 tsp lime
- 200ml reduced-fat coconut milk
- Juice of 1 lemon or lime
- Fresh herbs, to serve
- Optional chilli flakes or sliced jalapeños, added after serving

### Method
1. Heat olive oil in a large pan and cook onion for 6–8 minutes.
2. Add garlic and spices and cook for 1 minute.
3. Add tuna, cherry tomato and pumpkin, stirring to coat in the flavour base.
4. Add tomatoes, stock or water as listed and simmer until tender.
5. Cook or stir in the pearl barley as appropriate for the dish.
6. Season lightly and serve with fresh herbs, with chilli or jalapeños added at the table.

### Why this works for THA
Quality tuna is paired with cherry tomato, pumpkin and pearl barley for a whole-food meal where protein is balanced by vegetables and fibre.

### Planner readability notes
- category: lunch
- contains tuna
- contains cherry tomato
- contains pumpkin
- contains pearl barley
- mild base recipe
