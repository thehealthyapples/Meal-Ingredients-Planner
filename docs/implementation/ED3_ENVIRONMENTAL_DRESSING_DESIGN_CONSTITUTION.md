## 0 · What this implementation is, and what it is not

**Objective.** Create the **Environmental Dressing Design Constitution** — the single, permanent visual and material language against which **every future dressing object must be admitted** before one byte of visible dressing is ever shown. It is architecture and design governance only.

**What it is.** A new governing **Layer-2 Experience Architecture document** at `docs/architecture/ENVIRONMENTAL_DRESSING_DESIGN_CONSTITUTION.md`, plus this report and the README index entry. The Constitution is **the design-language face of `LIVINGHOME2`** — the exact relationship `OHDB1` holds to the Experience Blueprint: `LIVINGHOME2` owns *what the dressing layer is and may do* (ED1–ED12); the Constitution owns *how an object in it must look and feel to belong in this house*. It closes the visual gap `LIVINGHOME2` § 11 recorded in one line — *"the rendering medium of dressing items … inherits `EXP3` Verdict 3's open illustration question unchanged and adds no position to it"* — but closes it **as a standard, not as an approval**.

**What it is not.** It is **not** an asset, and not the bowl of apples. It admits **no** object; it builds no renderer, seasonal state, schema, persistence, UI, or token. It creates **no** palette, colour, material, feeling, or design rule of its own — every such value stays with its existing owner and is cited. It does **not** resolve `EXP3` Verdict 3 (the medium question); that verdict stays the Home Owner's, and the Constitution supplies only the standard it is judged against.

### 0.1 The ordering decision — a design standard *before* the first object

The `ED2` runtime recommendation named the next step *"ED3 — Standing Welcome"* (the first visible item). This workstream inserts the **Design Constitution** ahead of it and renames the first-object step **ED4 — Standing Welcome Admission**. The reason is the one `LIVINGHOME2` § 11 and `EXP3` Verdict 3 both point at: **the first admitted object is the first byte of visible dressing the product has ever shown, and there is no written visual standard to admit it against.** Admitting the bowl of apples first would set the house's dressing aesthetic *by that one object's execution* rather than by a governed standard — precisely the "erosion by taste" `LIVINGHOME2` § 1.3 exists to prevent. The Constitution is therefore the lawful next act: **one design language written first, so the first object — and the tenth, a decade later — is judged against a standard instead of a mood.** This is the `TIME3` / `LIVINGHOME1` / `LIVINGHOME2` / `ED1` precedent (law and shape first) applied to the layer's *visual* dimension.

### 0.2 One recorded decision — the medium question is NOT resolved here

`EXP3` Verdict 3 is a *genuinely open* owner decision: whether a dressing truth may be rendered as **commissioned illustration** or only in the product's **existing media** (content photography, type, material tokens), with the recorded default being *existing media, lawful today with no approval*, and the *illustrated track gated on the owner's explicit approval recorded at first admission*. `EXP3` itself warns that assuming the answer *"would be legislating from an implementation document."* This Constitution therefore **states the quality standard that binds either medium** (never stock-photographic, never cartoon, never a claim, one coherent hand — § 8) and **routes the medium verdict to the Home Owner** (§ 20). It adds no position to Verdict 3; it gives the Home Owner a written standard to decide it against.

### 2.1 The two lists that are the whole Constitution

The Constitution compresses to two lists (§ 2), each made checkable per facet by the sections that follow. Environmental Dressing must feel **warm · calm · natural · crafted · lived-with · restrained · consistent with the existing house**. It must never feel **glossy · synthetic · generic · promotional · themed · gamified · ornamental for its own sake · like stock imagery · like a dashboard decoration · like fabricated household activity**. Every section is one of these two lists applied to one facet (material, texture, colour, light, scale, patina, composition, placement), and the two lists win any tie (§ 2).

### 2.2 The constitution decisions established

- **One canonical design language** — this document, the object-level visual admission standard, is the single place a dressing object's look is judged.
- **One Home Owner approval authority** — `HOMEOWNER1`, cited and not duplicated; environmental-dressing approval is explicitly its seat.
- **One admission standard** — § 21's eleven-item admission evidence; an object missing any item is not admitted.
- **No duplicate aesthetic governance** — every colour/token/material/feeling stays with its owner (§ 1 table); the Constitution assembles, never re-owns.
- **The Constitution extends `LIVINGHOME2`** as its design-language face; it replaces nothing and cites ED1–ED12 throughout.
- **Environmental Dressing remains claim-free** — the standard *strengthens* ED3 by refusing photographic realism and fabricated household activity precisely because they edge toward a claim.
- **Household data, Companion behaviour and business logic remain untouched** — reaffirmed by citation (ED12; `LIVINGHOME2` § 7.4), altered nowhere.
