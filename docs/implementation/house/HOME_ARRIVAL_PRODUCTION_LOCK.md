## 3. The four decisions, realised in one room

### 3.1 Architecture — Concept B, permanent

Glass above, oak sill between, plaster room below, doors along the floor. The window is **real joinery**:
a head reveal with wall-thickness above the glass, jambs returning down each side, three bays held by
timber mullions with a lit inner edge and a cast shadow — *structure holding glass up*, not bars drawn on
a photo. This is the R2 defence NORTH4 named: the sill and mullions are **load-bearing in perpetuity**, so
no future hand can "tidy" the Home into a hero banner. **Nothing the household reads ever crosses the sill
onto the glass** — verified programmatically on every render.

### 3.2 Interior — The Kept House

No object was added to the room; only time was let pass over it. The oak of the sill and mullions is
**darkened the way timber darkens after years of the same morning sun**; the plaster carries a faint
hand-troweled grain (the one grain of The Linen Calm); the sill's front lip is **worn a shade lighter**
where hands and cups have rested; the room is **warmed a half-step at the sill** and the mullions lay soft
long shadows on the wall below — *the room receiving the morning* (The Warm Hour), never a second sun on
the orchard (Blueprint § 16). The interface recedes: the band is gone, the Companion is a line on the
wall, the console is hushed to a quiet serif italic with air around it. **The feeling comes from *time*,
which is the one interior quality that cannot be staged — only accumulated.**

### 3.3 Brand — the Pressed Apple in the wall

The prominent "The Healthy Apples" band is **retired** — the top of the room is empty plaster, and *that
emptiness is the brand decision made visible*. The sole mark is the **THA apple pressed a medium-shallow
relief into the open plaster** to the right, at eye height, in clear wall: a three-layer intaglio (ambient
occlusion, a recessed face with the light pooling toward the lit lower lip, and a two-rim relief — **dark
rim up, lit rim down**, lit by the room's one morning). It holds **no pigment** — tone-on-tone; *it is the
wall, not an image on it.* It is **discovered the moment the eye leaves the orchard**, and after six months
of mornings it is no longer a brand at all but a detail of *their* wall. The mark reads on an ordinary
screen (measured contrast well above the BRAND2 visibility floor) yet never dominates — the exact whisper
BRAND2 § 6 specified.

### 3.4 Companion — the apple, carved into a sage disc

The friend at the counter is **the house's own mark**: the canonical THA apple pressed deep into a soft
sage-green ceramic disc (a lit top rim, a shaded lower body, a soft contact shadow — *furniture, not a
sticker*). It is **calm at rest** — no halo, no badge, no motion — and sits in the **same corner in every
room** (Experience Blueprint § 14), 48 px, above the doors on mobile. It is **always available and never
visually dominant.** Its voice is the dissolved Companion line on the wall — one warm, true, data-borne
sentence, verbatim from the Notice Engine, quieter when the day is quiet (Core Principle 6). *(The aware
breathing light is deliberately **not** shown here — it is held behind the one governance amendment COMP1
§ 10 proposes; the shipping baseline is the calm idle button.)*

## 4. The two honest days

Arrival has two weathers of the household's own life — driven by whether the day is anchored (CONV1 P8) —
and **the view never moves between them**; only how much of the same orchard is opened changes.

| | **The full day** *(a planned day)* | **The quiet day** *(the default — 192 / 195 households)* |
|---|---|---|
| The window | opens to its full E3 generosity | the blind lowers one level — more wall carries the arrival |
| The console | the real facts, in the Kept House's quiet hand (*three planned · nothing to fetch · 28 of 30 this week*) | three honest absences, said with dignity (*a clear morning · list is clear · blossom this week*) |
| The Companion | *"Three meals planned — and nothing left to fetch."* | *"The orchard is in blossom — a good week for something green and simple."* |
| Reads as | a good morning, already planned | **a quiet morning, clear and calm** |
| Never reads as | — | **"nobody home"** — the trap this design closes |

**Build the quiet day first.** It is the day almost every household actually arrives in, and it is now the
strong one: a calm morning, never a grid of zeroes.

## 6. Small production polish (before implementation)

The design is complete; these are the *finishing* notes the implementer should carry into the real code —
small, named, and none of them a redesign:

1. **The pressed apple is a rendered specification, not a shipped material.** These renders approximate
   plaster relief with masked gradient layers. In the product, build the emboss from the room's **actual
   light model** on the `.home-arrival` wall (a masked pseudo-element with the two-rim deboss and the lime
   fill) and **re-judge on real devices in daylight** — the visibility floor shifts with the final
   material (BRAND2 § 7). The honest lever if it under-reads in the wild is **more depth or size, never
   pigment.**
2. **The Companion carve and the aware light are two changes, not one.** Ship the **calm carved-apple
   emblem** now (visual-only, one file — COMP1 § 11); hold the **aware breathing light** behind the
   § 320 amendment (COMP1 § 10) and wire its `useCompanionNotices` presence signal as a separate,
   behaviour change.
3. **The full day is eased one notch on desktop.** The generous E3 window is set to `--sill-y: 372px` on
   desktop (not 398) so the fuller day's room clears the floor at a 900 px viewport — the defect NORTH4
   caught, closed here. Keep this value; do not restore the taller sill without re-checking the fold.
4. **The retired band leaves an empty plaster shelf at the top.** That emptiness is intentional. If the
   real shell must carry an account/settings affordance, keep it **tone-on-tone and to one corner** — it
   must never become a second brand line competing with the pressed apple.
5. **The dissolved Companion line keeps one quiet affordance.** The "View suggestion ›" link is retained
   as the only inline call to action; if interface density must fall further, it is the first thing to go
   (the sage Companion button is the always-available path).
6. **The orchard's sun (§ 7) is still open — and above this document.** The lock composes by crop (the
   sanctioned status quo, NORTH4 D3); the mirror-vs-amend-vs-crop decision on the upper-right flare, the
   Orchard Palette (D1), and the signature font (D4) remain **owner/platform decisions** and do not block
   adopting this composition.
