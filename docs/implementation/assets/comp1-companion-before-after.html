Before

Before — a flat apple silhouette at 58%, shallow two-line emboss, and a halo that surrounds the outside of the button.

After

After — the canonical apple carved to ~80% with a deep intaglio recess; the warm light escapes from within the apple itself.

What changed

  1. Sole identity. The single canonical THA apple — the same mark used across the product — is now the whole button, not an icon placed on a green disc.
  2. Scale. The apple grew from ~58% to ~80% of the disc, keeping a balanced sage rim; the button reads as the emblem, not a container.
  3. Depth. A three-layer intaglio — contact-shadow, recess face, and interior gradient — carves the apple into the material instead of printing it on top.
  4. Light. The arch's one morning catches the upper rim while the recess pools a realistic shadow; a soft catch-light rides the lower lip.
  5. Halo. The aware light is now apple-shaped and blooms from within the carved mark, spilling softly past the edge — no longer a ring around the outside.
  6. Unchanged. Sage ceramic material, 48px target, placement, focus ring, calm idle, and all Companion behaviour — untouched. No badges, no text, no new colours.
Faithful, not a mock: both discs use THA's real material tokens (plaster, sage --primary, the arch's warm morning) and the real canonical apple mask, so this is a true preview of the refinement. The After disc is the design carried in comp1-companion-prototype.html. Shown at 64px for legibility; product size is 48px. Presence is never urgency — no count, no badge, no status colour.
