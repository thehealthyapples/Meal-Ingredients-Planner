## 1. The headline

Three facts dominate everything else, and none of them is a code-quality problem.

**1. None of this is in production.** `main` is **108 commits behind** the working branch and was last touched 2026-06-30. Production auto-deploys from `main` (GitHub → Render, per `RELEASE.md` and `.replit`). `main` contains **no CI workflow and none of the TRUST1 security tests**. The entire Production Trust programme, REL1–3, OPS1, PHASE5A–E and PX1 exist only on a feature branch, **30 commits of which are not pushed to `origin`**. Production today runs code that predates the security programme: no auth rate limiting, no fail-closed session secret, no secure-cookie flag.

**2. The product's core promise renders nothing.** Verified against the live database:

| Evidence edge | Total | Cited | Signed off |
|---|---|---|---|
| Composition (food→nutrient) | 1,988 | 49 | **0** |
| Bridge (nutrient→benefit) | 70 | — | 21 (5 benefits, **0 named reviewers**) |

The render gate (`server/services/nutrition-knowledge-registry.ts:220-223`) correctly requires *both* edges signed. Composition has zero, so **every benefit chip on every surface renders empty**. This is the trust gate working exactly as designed — KNOW5 took 755 chips to 0 rather than ship claims resting on an AI-drafted premise — but it means the household-facing nutrition knowledge is currently blank, and it is unblocked by *one command and one named nutritionist*, not by engineering.

**3. ~3,865 lines of work across eight workstreams are uncommitted.** HHP2, HHP3, HNP1, PANTRY1, PLAN2, SHOP1, CBK2, NTC-P2 and the PDA1 audit exist only in the working tree — never committed on any branch reachable from `HEAD`, preserved only incidentally inside dangling snapshot commits that are not ancestors of `HEAD`. Meanwhile `deploy.sh` runs `git add -A`, commits, **runs no tests**, and pushes straight to `main`.

**What is genuinely excellent:** `npm test` passes **77 suites, zero failures**; the adoption gate passes (64 checks, 1 notice, 0 failed); the Intelligence platform is real and wired; and the knowledge trust gate is enforced in code and in CI. The engineering is strong. The *shipping* and the *content* are not.

### 🔴 Production Foundation — 50%, High, actively in progress

**Completed.** All ten TRUST1/REL/OPS/CBK controls are real, fail closed, and tested in CI: session secret fails closed (`server/auth.ts:134-160`, `server/index.ts:106-114`), Postgres-backed auth rate limiting with 12 policies that production cannot disable (`server/lib/auth-rate-limit.ts`), secure production cookies, meal-ownership/IDOR protection, a fail-closed schema-push guard (`scripts/db/schema-push-guard.ts`), durable media storage (OPS1), canonical cookbook seeding (CBK1), and a genuine CI pipeline with an ephemeral Postgres service (`.github/workflows/ci.yml`).

**Remaining.** The enforcement layer was never finished:
- `verify:deployment-config` **fails today** — `.replit` carries an uncommitted tooling edit. REL2's blocker has silently regressed because nothing runs the gate.
- **Branch protection is not applied.** `scripts/ci/branch-protection.json` states plainly that *"nothing in this repository ever sends it"*, and `verify:branch-protection` is wired into neither CI nor `release:check`.
- **CI runs 3 of the 7 gates.** `release:check` chains six; `ci.yml` runs three. `verify:branch-protection`, `verify:schema-coverage`, `verify:cookbook-seed` and `verify:prod` gate nothing automatically.
- **Migration coverage is 50%** (46/92 tables). A new table works in dev via `db:push` and 500s in production.
- **No backups, no tested restore (O5), no error tracking (O2), no structured logging (O4), no health endpoint, no alerting, no helmet/CSP.** All verified absent.
- **`deploy.sh` hardening (O7) never started.** It runs no tests, `git add -A`s a 107-entry dirty tree, and pushes to `main`.

**Dependencies.** Circular and unrecorded: merge to `main` → `ci.yml` exists there → the required status check can report → branch protection becomes applicable → `deploy.sh`'s push fails loudly → O7 hardens it. Nothing can be enforced on `main` until `main` has the workflow.

### 🟢 Companion Evolution — 70% current-gen / 20% end-state, High, actively in progress

**This inverts the expectation.** The Companion is **the most heavily built system in the repository** — 30 modules in `server/intelligence/conversation/`, including a 67 KB gateway, a 32 KB behaviour engine, a 29 KB notice engine and a 29 KB personality registry; roughly 40 of the ~80 test scripts target it.

**Dependencies.** None external. The next increment — the Goals capability — is greenfield, precisely specified (`THA_FOOD_INTELLIGENCE_PLATFORM_ARCHITECTURE.md` §7.2), and **ready to begin today**.

## 4. Re-prioritised roadmap

**The Master Evolution Roadmap (2026-06-18) should no longer be the plan of record.** Its editorial-critical-path thesis has been **proven exactly right** — Risk R11 (*"editorial capacity is the real pole"*) has come true, and it is the binding constraint today. But its factual assessments have drifted (§6).

### The next three highest-value workstreams

**1. Release & Enforcement — ship what is already built.**
Nothing else matters until this lands: 108 commits including every security control are invisible to households, and production is running *pre-TRUST1* code. Commit the 3,865 uncommitted lines (they are one `git add -A` away from being swept into a deploy, and one disk failure from being lost); merge to `main`; fix `.replit`; apply branch protection; make CI run `release:check` rather than a subset — **it would have caught the `.replit` regression this audit found by hand**; harden `deploy.sh` (O7). This is the precondition for all nine other programmes.

**2. Sign the knowledge — the cheapest unlock in the repository.**
One command (`npm run knowledge:signoff -- --edge composition --reviewer "<named nutritionist>"`) over the 49 cited composition claims turns **0 benefit chips into 48 across 29 foods**, and lights up three surfaces simultaneously. Then extend cited composition from 29 foods toward the foods households actually see. Also fix the 21 anonymous sign-offs — the DB cannot currently evidence *who* signed, so no claim can be audited or withdrawn. **This needs a named human, not an engineer.**

**3. Close the launch Definition of Done.**
Three pure-engineering items, blocked on nothing: build the `/weekly-nutrition` route (the one missing launch surface); reopen and actually fix the error-as-empty P0 (12 household pages tell people "you have nothing" when a request fails); run the a11y + contrast audit the roadmap has called a launch blocker since June.

**Then, in sequence:** TRUST1 Phase 1 (privacy notice, T&Cs, erasure, Art. 9 consent — plus backups, tested restore, error tracking, health endpoint) as the hard gate on Commercial Launch. Run the Companion benchmark at HEAD with the judge tier enabled, since intelligence quality is currently unmeasured. Register the orphaned Tier-4 test. Defer Community until its product question is answered.

## 5. Maturity estimates

**Overall platform maturity: ~62%.** The engineering platform is mature and, in places, exceptional — the knowledge trust gate, the Adoption Register and the Intelligence capability model are better than most production systems carry. The gap is not capability; it is that the platform's *outputs* (shipped code, signed knowledge, measured quality) lag its *inputs* badly.

**Production readiness: ~40%.** Application security is ~85% and well-engineered. The operational envelope is ~15%: no backups, no tested restore, no monitoring, no error tracking, no health endpoint, 50% migration coverage, an unhardened deploy path, and six of seven gates wired to nothing. And **zero percent of it is deployed.**

**Launch readiness: ~20%** for a public, paid launch: no legal basis, no payments, no analytics, no upgrade path, zero renderable health benefits, one of five launch surfaces missing. For a **soft, free, invite-only launch the figure is materially higher (~50%)** — the blockers there are knowledge sign-off and the error-state P0, not commerce.

**The through-line:** THA has built governance honest enough to tell the truth about itself, and the truth it is telling is that the work is finished, un-shipped, and waiting on two human signatures — a nutritionist's, and whoever presses merge.
