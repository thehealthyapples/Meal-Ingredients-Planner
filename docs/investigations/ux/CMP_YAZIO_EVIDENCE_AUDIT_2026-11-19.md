## Product and THA observations

**Verified:** Yazio's Pro feature set has meaningful overlap with personal nutrition journeys: photo-assisted tracking, flexible future meal planning, advanced nutrient detail, weekly/multi-year analysis, mood/reflection logging, product ratings and grocery lists. Two relevant differentiators are personalized Meal Tips after a diary entry and barcode comparison between two products.

**Verified:** Yazio's custom recipe guide currently warns about a feature issue and says user-created recipes are stored locally and may not sync to other devices. This is relevant when assessing THA's own capture workflow: distinguish local drafts from household-owned shared recipes and communicate sync status clearly.

**SUGGESTIONS — internal only:** assess whether THA's Nutrition Boost should also offer a reviewed, contextual post-diary suggestion (for example, an optional protein/fibre/fruit/vegetable idea), reusing the existing Nutrition Boost owner and honoring the household's restrictions. Also use Yazio's product-to-product barcode comparison as a prompt to evaluate THA's existing Analyser comparison flow. These are research leads, not public claims or approved feature changes.

**Evidence limits:** no account/device testing was performed. Yazio labels many findings Pro-only and flags current issues in custom meal/recipe flows; actual feature availability can vary by version/platform. No feature was marked No due to documentation silence.
