## Product observations

**Verified:** Lifesum's strongest overlap is personal nutrition tracking: barcode comparison, multi-modal AI logging (photo, voice and text), body measurements, nutrient detail, a weekly Life Score and structured multi-day programs. Its shopping list is tied to meal-plan recipes and can be exported, while family meal planning and eater-specific restriction handling are not established by reviewed sources.

**Evidence limitation:** Lifesum's public feature page and help center were used; no account/device testing was performed. Meal plans are described as Premium in help articles. The exact subscription access for all nutrition views and the current regional/platform feature matrix were not resolved. Weekly Life Score must not be conflated with a full weekly consumed-nutrient report.
