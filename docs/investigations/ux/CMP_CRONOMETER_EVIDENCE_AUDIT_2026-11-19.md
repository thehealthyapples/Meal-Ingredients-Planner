## Product and THA observations

**Verified:** Cronometer is especially deep in individual nutrient tracking: its diary, configurable nutrient targets, daily scores, period-average reports, barcode/photo/voice logging, food suggestions and personalized Coach tools are documented. Its Gold recipe URL importer and reusable custom recipes broaden food entry without demonstrating a weekly household planner or shopping-list flow.

**SUGGESTIONS — internal only:** Evaluate whether THA can make its “add nutrition” strength more visible and actionable in the weekly nutrition view: identify a specific nutrient gap across recent meals, suggest suitable household-compatible additions, and let people choose an option without turning nutrition into subtraction or judgment. Reuse existing nutrition/AI guidance and restriction owners; do not create a second assistant or parallel nutrition state. Cronometer’s explicit nutrient targets and editable food suggestions provide a useful benchmark. This is a research candidate, not a public claim or approved feature commitment.

**Source limits:** Gold/free feature boundaries are noted where established. Some sources describe current help content, but actual behavior by country, device and account was not tested. No feature is marked No solely from documentation silence.
