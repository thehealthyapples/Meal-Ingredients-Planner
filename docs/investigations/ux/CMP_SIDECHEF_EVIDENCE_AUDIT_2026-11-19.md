## Product and THA observations

**Verified:** SideChef is a strong planning/cooking/shopping competitor: family-size personalized weekly planning, allergy/diet inputs, recipe serving adjustment, grocery lists and retailer ordering, substantial guided-cooking support, recipes from pantry items and AI ingredient substitution. It explicitly says meal plans and lists are not shared. Nutrition is mainly recipe-level in reviewed sources; photo-to-recipe AI and some scanner details have Beta/rollout ambiguity.

**SUGGESTIONS — internal only:** Benchmark THA's household restrictions and recipe adaptation against SideChef's plan-level allergies plus AI substitution; confirm that THA can show which member a change serves. Review pantry discovery and whether scanned/manual stock updates remain trustworthy after cooking. Consider improving recipe-step guidance and retailer-aware basket review. Keep any AI changes within existing THA capability/permission owners, and retain the THA household-first distinction. These are investigation candidates, not public promises.

**Source limits:** Some SideChef scanner and AI pages are business-facing product material rather than step-by-step consumer help, and RecipeGen is an older Beta announcement. The FAQ states that meal plans and grocery lists are not shared. Retail ordering is fulfilled by grocery partners and matched products/availability vary. No app was installed or hands-on tested; no No is inferred from silence.
