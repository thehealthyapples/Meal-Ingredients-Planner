# Mealia comparison evidence audit

**Checked:** 19 November 2026
**Scope:** All 30 tasks in the THA comparison matrix. Sources: Mealia's official UK product/help pages and its UK Apple App Store listing. These are first-party descriptions, not independent or hands-on verification. The App Store listing describes the supported supermarket basket; the web product site currently lists Tesco, Asda, Sainsbury's and Morrisons. Exact availability may differ by region/platform.
**Evidence rule:** Yes = documented; Some limits = documented with scope or qualification; Not reported = no sufficient finding in reviewed developer sources, not proof of absence.

## Overall observation

**Verified from first-party descriptions:** Mealia's strongest documented capability is the budget-to-supermarket path: choose a weekly budget and servings, select recipes, and hand a populated basket to a supported UK supermarket. It describes household-level dietary preferences and portion scaling, recipe imports from websites, and recipe planning. Its current store-page and website descriptions differ in how many supermarket integrations they list, so the public row is explicitly scoped to the UK product claim and checkout remains retailer-side.

**Inferred:** Mealia is a useful THA benchmark for connecting meal decisions to the full grocery shop. The reviewed evidence does not establish per-eater restriction profiles, in-app nutritional analysis, or diary breadth. These remain evidence gaps; they do not establish that Mealia lacks the functions.

**Evidence quality limitation:** Most capability claims come from Mealia's own product pages and App Store description; there was no independent feature test or user account review. No market ranking was performed.
