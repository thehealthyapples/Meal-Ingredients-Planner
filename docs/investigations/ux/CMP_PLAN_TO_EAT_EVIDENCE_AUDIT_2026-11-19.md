## Product and THA observations

**Verified:** Plan to Eat's strongest overlap spans the complete recipe-to-plan-to-shop path, plus social/paper recipe capture, reusable Menus, portion scaling, freezer batch cooking and guided cooking. Its newer Food Logger also tracks actual food intake against calorie and macro goals and provides a weekly chart; the official release notes call the logger Beta. Its documented Freezer adjusts shopping quantities, tracks portions, and places a defrost reminder before a frozen meal is planned.

**SUGGESTIONS — internal only:** (1) Compare THA's recipe photo/OCR capture against Plan to Eat's recipe review/edit flow and multi-page handling. (2) Assess THA's “Add to Freezer” handoff against the competitor's batch portion count, ingredient-list adjustment, remaining frozen-meal quantity and thaw reminder. (3) Consider whether the THA food diary and weekly nutrition analysis can make the distinction between planned versus actually eaten meals as clear as Plan to Eat's Planner/Food Log split. Preserve THA's household and positive nutrition-addition approach. These are research candidates, not public copy or commitments.

**Source limits:** The Food Logger was described as Beta in the 6 November 2026 release notes. The recipe nutrition calculator acknowledges that its calculated values still need improvement. The international grocery handoff uses a third-party integration and requires users to review transferred brands/quantities. No No rating is based solely on documentation silence.
