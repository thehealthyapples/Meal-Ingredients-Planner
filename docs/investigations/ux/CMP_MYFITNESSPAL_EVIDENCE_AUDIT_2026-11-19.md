# MyFitnessPal comparison evidence audit

**Checked:** 19 November 2026
**Scope:** All 30 tasks in the existing THA comparison matrix, using MyFitnessPal's official help centre only. This is a source review, not hands-on testing.
**Region/platform note:** Some key plan capabilities are officially listed for US, UK, Canada, Ireland, New Zealand and Australia, in English, on iOS/Android app version 25.10.0 or newer. Grocery-app synchronization is narrower: US and Canada.
**Legend:** Yes = documented; Some limits = documented with scope/tier or capability limits; Not reported = no sufficient finding in reviewed developer sources, not proof of absence.

## Overall observation

**Verified:** MyFitnessPal is stronger in personal diary logging, barcode/photo logging, calorie/macro reporting, weight measurements, and weekly progress summaries. Premium+ now adds household-sized planning, dietary settings and a grocery list, so the comparison should not describe it as tracking-only. Its documented household controls are at plan level, and it does not establish separate restriction profiles for each eater. Planning and shopping have subscription, language, platform and regional conditions.

**Not established:** The reviewed official help materials do not establish paper-plan capture, external plan import, social recipe import, mood/energy journaling, or AI recipe tailoring. These stay neutral grey on the public grid and should not be treated as confirmed absences.

**Quality boundary:** These findings describe what official help pages document, not comparative accuracy, usability, or whether every account sees a feature. No competitor account was used.
