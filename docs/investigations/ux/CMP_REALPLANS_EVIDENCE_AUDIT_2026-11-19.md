# Real Plans comparison evidence audit

**Checked:** 19 November 2026
**Scope:** All 30 tasks in the existing THA comparison matrix, using official Real Plans product and support sources. This is a documentation review, not hands-on account testing. Where the current site says nutrition data or macro tracking needs a separate subscription, that distinction is retained. “Not reported” means the reviewed sources do not establish the capability; it is not proof of absence.

## Product and THA observations

**Verified:** Real Plans' clearest strengths are family-size-aware weekly planning, declared diet/ingredient exclusions, recipe capture and editing, a plan-generated grocery list, and cooking timeline/mode. Nutrition depth and macro tracking are separate from its standard subscription. Household-scale planning should not be conflated with separate eater profiles or per-eater adaptations.

**Verified:** Its official Macro Tracker page says the planning flow reminds users to take meat out of the freezer; the FAQ also encourages batch cooking and freezing portions. These suggest THA could connect its existing “Add to Freezer” inventory/action to batch-cooking plans, scheduled thaw reminders and the meals/portions produced. This is a development opportunity to assess, not a public comparison claim or approved implementation.

**Source caveat:** Real Plans' pages differ on recipe counts and some prices; neither is needed for this feature comparison. No account/device testing was performed. Capability wording and availability can change by plan, device and region.
