## Problem

The current planner is a 6-week rolling canvas.

Planner content is not retained historically.

When weeks are cleared, replaced, or reset, previous planner history is lost.

As a result, THA cannot currently determine:

* Often used ingredients
* Long-term household familiarity
* Seasonal cooking patterns
* Nutrition progression over time

---

## Opportunity

Introduce a future Planner Archive capability for Premium users.

The archive would preserve historical planner activity and support:

### Household Familiarity

Examples:

* Pumpkin Seeds
* Basil
* Lentils
* Mixed Beans

THA could identify ingredients frequently used by a household over time.

### Seasonal Learning

Examples:

* Summer meal patterns
* Winter meal patterns
* School holiday meal plans
* Christmas meal plans

### Nutrition Progression

Examples:

* Plant diversity growth
* Ingredient variety growth
* Household nutrition enrichment trends

### Historical Planner Access

Examples:

* View previous plans
* Reuse successful plans
* Restore seasonal plans

---

## Important Principle

This capability exists to support:

> Nutritional enrichment through household learning.

It is not intended to become a complex inventory system.

THA should continue prioritising:

* Simplicity
* Practicality
* Household adoption
* Nutrition enhancement
