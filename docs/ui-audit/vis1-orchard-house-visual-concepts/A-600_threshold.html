⚑ The noun collision — one house, two thresholds, opposite directions

SHOP2 earns the noun honestly: “Shopping is not the market. It is the threshold before it.” But TRANSLATION1 § 4.1 already owns THRESHOLD as the arrival. The mission's noun is kept and the collision is drawn. Naming is not this board's to settle — and HOUSE4 § 5.2 already found this house's spatial vocabulary is spent: “The canon has a hall it cannot name, a doorway that means something else, a threshold that never leaves the room, and a corridor that is only ever an insult.” This is the fifth spent word, found on the sixth board.

And “Market Basket” was refused, three times over "A market is somewhere you are; this room is somewhere you leave from. The whole design — E1 light, one note, brisk, ‘passed through, not lingered in’ — descends from that, and ‘market’ inverts every line of it." Second: "the shop is off-stage… there is no chapter in which the household is at the market, because THA never goes there." Third: in economics a market basket is "a fixed basket of goods used to measure prices — a measurement instrument… The household's shopping is not an index."

1 · Emotional intention

Brisk, satisfying, momentary — a room passed through, not lingered in. There is momentum here, and it is welcome.

OHDB § 13.5 · Orchard Living Book, Preparing To Leave For The Shop

Momentum is the only register in the house the canon actively encourages — every other room is calm, unhurried, or at rest. And it is bounded exactly:

The bound on momentum "Nothing counts down, nothing expires for effect, nothing hurries the person toward a decision" (Experience Language) → “momentum is the household's, never the product's. A room that moves with a person who is already moving is brisk. A room that makes them move is urgency.”

This room is defined by a negation: Shopping is defined by how quickly you leave it. Every other room's completion resolves at rest; this one resolves out the door.

2 · Light, and the relationship to the orchard

The Blueprint wrote its empty-state law with this room in the frame Blueprint § 6.2 rule 2 states the exception using this room's own example: "An honestly empty working room may breathe at E2 — the view beyond a clear list is exactly the right calm." The Living Book agrees in its own voice: "on a clear day, when the list is short and honest, the room can afford to breathe a little and let a bit more of the morning in."
Live — a second orchard, built out of an img and a backgroundImage The orchard renders globally and inside the room's own writing surface, with a non-theme-aware rgba(255,255,255,0.80) tint. Three divergent materials sit in one file. "It is a second orchard by the back door… Shopping has built one. Not as a metaphor — as an <img> and a backgroundImage, stacked." Blueprint § 16's costume: “Shopping added three of its own.”

3 · Materials, and the ground's posture

The list is the room "The Planner has a table that holds a week; the Cookbook has a shelf that holds a book; Shopping has nothing but the list. There is no furniture here that survives the list's absence" — a material rule that makes the room a function of its content. Hence: “A room whose only material is its list, arriving with no list on it, has not arrived badly — it has arrived as a different room.”

One note by the door, sized to exactly what's on it and no more. The materials are as simple as that sentence. This is the plainest room in the house and the one room where plainness is the specification rather than a risk.

But the distance between a hallway and a form is "exactly the width of the word competent":

A hallway is plain and you are glad to be in it. A form is plain and you are not.

4 · Depth

The shallowest and smallest ground in the set: one note, one plane, nothing behind it but working brightness. There is no depth budget to spend here, and none is wanted — you are on your way out.

The coldness problem, in its hardest form Shopping is the one room the canon wants plain. "There is no warmth budget to restore." The Cookbook has food; the Pantry has light and language; Shopping has neither. "The room's entire defence against sterility is one word — competent — and the household's own list. Strip the second orchard and what must remain is not warmth but competence: the list, legible, correct, and ready."

The Companion at the threshold — the second of its two quoted lines

The Companion is the friend who catches you at the threshold — “you're out of the thing you use every week” — offered once, easy to take or leave. It helps you leave prepared; it never holds you at the door.

Orchard Living Book, Preparing To Leave For The Shop

"‘Never holds you at the door’ is the room's first principle enforced against the one actor with a motive to break it: a Companion that helps you leave is a friend; a Companion that has one more thing to show you before you go is a checkout queue."

Every added tap must buy the person something — the person, not the product. Every temptation (price comparison, a better-product card, a savings total) "does not make them wrong. It makes them chargeable."

The grammar test for anything this room says: "Could a kind, knowledgeable friend say this sentence to your face, about a food you just chose, without it sounding like a correction? If not, it is banned."

The one place THA is permitted to raise its voice The allergen shopping-restriction-conflict — the platform's sole critical emitter, structurally protected. “The one place THA is permitted to raise its voice is in the room where the household is about to spend money on something that could hurt someone.” This is the sanctioned exception to colour never alarms outside genuine safety, and it lives here.

7 · The one sign of life

The crossing-off — struck and settled into shade; no celebration per item.

Blueprint § 12.2

✓ The set's cleanest pass — met to the word line-through text-muted-foreground, transition-colors — a colour fade, no transform, no height animation — and the mutation has no onSuccess handler at all. "‘line-through text-muted-foreground’ is literally struck and shaded, and the mutation has no success path to celebrate from. The ceiling is not merely respected — it is met to the word, and the room could not celebrate an item if it wanted to." No confetti library exists in the codebase.

Of the four Living Details in this set: the Planner's is unimplementable, the Cookbook's is half-sourced and fabricating the other half, the Pantry's is dead. This one works.

The sharpest delight paradox in the house The canon names only two qualifying premium moments — "a first plan made, a first list completed" — one of them is this room's, and then it forbids the obvious way to mark it. The resolution: "even a qualifying moment stays inside THA's calm register: acknowledgement, not celebration theatre." →

“The permitted expression of THA's most-earned moment is that the list is simply, quietly, done — and the room lets you leave.”

The one live line to watch: “Great shop — nothing left to find.” "It is not theatre, and it is not acknowledgement either — it is a compliment… a household finishing a shop would recognise the milestone and would not describe it as great."

8 · What this room must never become

Refused

  • The upsell at the finish line. Never “upgrade”, “share”, or whatever the business currently wishes people did more. “A room with a checkout and one rule about commerce should treat that rule as load-bearing rather than as a formality.”
  • A place that asks you to linger
  • All view, no room — a full landscape behind a short list
  • The scold at the door. Live: THA tells a household its banana is “Industrial” — in red, about a banana THA never scored. "A correction — of a food the household already chose, at the moment they are trying to leave."
  • Confetti or celebration for finishing a list
  • Marketing gimmicks in a functional workspace. “The working product is never a campaign.”

Entry routes to watch

  • Every good idea this room will ever have. "This is the anti-pattern most likely to be reasoned into, because every step is defensible and only the sum is wrong."
  • The retailer relationship
  • The Companion with one more thing to show you
  • Reaching for warmth to fix plainness — there is no warmth budget; reach for competence

The test

  • Does this help them leave prepared, or does it hold them at the door?
  • Whose momentum is this — theirs, or ours?
  • Does this tap buy them something?

Shopping is not the market. It is the threshold before it — the list gathered by the door, in the plainest light in the house, on a note sized to exactly what's on it and no more. Its register is momentum, the only room the canon lets move. Its whole grace is that you leave it: keys, bag, list, done.
