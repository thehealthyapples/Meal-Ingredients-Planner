PLATE 01

Desktop Home — the room entire

Concept · widest horizon

The fullest expression of the three grounds. The orchard is the largest thing and the quietest thing — area, not attention. The extra width of a large screen becomes air and view, never a wider dashboard. The counter stays compact and centred so the view keeps its share. Ground · behind

The orchard. Setting, never decoration. Oriented-toward, never contained. Constant across every room. TRANSLATION1 §4.1

The counter. One solid lit oak plane, never nested. The only layer that changes room to room. TRANSLATION1 §4.2 KITCHEN ISLAND

Ground · front

Companion & overlays. What genuinely floats — elevation reserved for these alone. EXPBLUE §8.1

The law of depth

Depth means distance, not drama. One soft, warm, single-direction shadow; no parallax. TRANSLATION1 §5.4

PLATE 05

Mobile Home — the room in the hand

Concept · the primary design

Designed mobile-first, because that is where most households arrive. PLATE 06

Typography — the three voices

PLATE 07

PLATE 08

Companion — one fixed chair

Placement & conduct

The knowledgeable friend at the counter — a presence, not a room. One fixed chair, lower-right foreground, at every hour and in every room, so the household never hunts for it. Its entire sign of life is timing: it arrives a beat after you. No face, no pulse, no typing dots, no simulated mood. TRANSLATION1 §4.4 COMPANION · EXPBLUE §13 · UIA §10

Waits — silence is a valid state; speaks only when something is worth saying. EXPLANG §5.7
Filling every silence to seem useful. EXPARCH §11
Offers once, then puts the attention back; the choice stays the household's. EXPARCH P5

The person crosses first; the Companion joins after. EXPBLUE §12.2

Lit as part of the room. EXPBLUE §13

PLATE 09

Motion — lighting, not light shows

Notes · what may move

The one test, quotable in any review: if the person notices the animation before the content, the animation has failed. Home moves almost never, and only to mean something. Exactly two motions are sanctioned; everything else is still. EXPLANG P.H · EXPBLUE §9 · UIA §11 · HOUSE1 §14

1 · The one light moment

Morning sun settling on the counter — once a day, on arrival. Reads as light landing on a surface, softly. Returns and the rest of the day have none; the room is simply, already, there. ORCHARD2 §5.2 · HOUSE1 §7

2 · The Companion's arrival beat

You cross first; the friend takes its chair a beat behind. Manners rendered as timing — its entire sign of life. No pulse, no dots. EXPBLUE §12.2 · §13

Still by construction

— no movement —

The shell never moves. The orchard never moves. Living Details never animate. Place survives total stillness; the view's life is warmth and light, never movement. EXPBLUE §6.1·§12.5·§14

Reduced-motion fully honoured — with motion off, Home is already there, complete, losing nothing. The arrival is felt, not watched. UIA §11 · ORCHARD2 §5

PLATE 10

Empty state — the kept quiet

State · primary, not edge

A well-run household on a calm day has little to report, so the quiet Home is not an edge case — it is a primary state, and it must be the warmest one. The deliberate quiet of a room someone keeps, not the emptiness of a room no one uses. This exact distinction is why the Kept Room was chosen over the cold Glass Pavilion. TRANSLATION1 §4.3 QUIET CORNERS · OHDB §4 · ORCHARD3 §5

The single guard against cold

A quiet Home is visibly lived in — the household's own name in the greeting, the orchard alive beyond the glass, the friend in the chair. EXPLANG §3A.4 · HOUSE1 §15

No door is fine

When there is no useful next step, Home offers none and stays complete. A calm day should feel calm, not be dressed into busyness. EXPBLUE §12.3

Never

Calm curdling into cold — the funeral parlour, the empty white room, the one thing THA must never be. EXPLANG §3A.1

A full-strength room with nothing in it must never read as "nobody home."

PLATE 11

Returning Home — the design of constancy

State · every entry after the first

A household enters Home many times a day. Every entry after the first is a return, not an arrival — and the design of a return is the design of constancy. The room is exactly as it was left: same warm morning, same still orchard, nothing rearranged to seem fresh. The greeting is not re-spent. HOUSE1 §8 · EXPBLUE §12.2 · OLB Returning Home

THE GOLD STANDARD

What every future room inherits

Home is entered first, returned to most

Home is the Entrance Hall, so its design is the household's sense of the whole house — which is exactly why it is the reference every room inherits. A future room takes the grammar, not the E3 horizon. HOUSE1 §22 · EXPBLUE §5

Inherited · always

Arrive before you work. Every room opens with its own miniature arrival — a calm posture before its first task. EXPLANG §5

Inherited · always

One warm working ground, one primary action, the friend a beat behind, constant walls. These do not change room to room. TRANSLATION1 §4

Inherited · always

One morning, one orchard, one Companion, one shell — forever. The constants Home establishes, every room keeps. EXPBLUE §5.2

Inherited · the principle only

Size the view to the room's purpose. Home spends the most (E3) because looking is its purpose; every other room spends less. The discipline is Home's gift; the level is each room's own. EXPBLUE §6.2

Get the entrance hall right and every room off it is already half-designed.

Design intent — not implementation, and not governing law. This plate-set applies the governing blueprints and owns only the concrete visual design of one room; it creates no rule and no second owner (EXPBLUE §18; Architecture Principle 2). Nothing here is a token, a component, or a colour value: every binding value remains the UI Architecture's (§7/§8/§9/§11), and the tonal drawings show composition, proportion and hierarchy only. The Kept Room's warm ground, lit oak counter and soft single-direction depth sit beyond the UI Architecture's current flat-surface law (UIA §4) and ship only once the governed UIA §4 amendment admits them, exposure & light enter as semantic tokens by admission (UIA §16), the greeting is admitted as its own Living Detail decision, and the Kept Room itself graduates by amendment. Two Blueprint open items bear directly on Home and must close first: the orchard's canonical owner and Home's header. This design declares that governance path (HOUSE1 §0.4 · TRANSLATION1 §2.4) — it does not jump it.
