# First-visit hints

> One dismissible sentence, shown once, on six of THA's surfaces — the entirety
> of THA's in-product guidance.

## What it is

A first-visit hint is a single dismissible sentence, shown once, on six of THA's
surfaces. Taken together, these six sentences are the *entirety* of THA's
in-product guidance — there is no tour, no coach-mark sequence, nothing that
follows the household from screen to screen. This is what actually waits when
onboarding promises "When you get there, we'll guide you."
