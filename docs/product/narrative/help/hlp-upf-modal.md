# What is UPF?

> Explains ultra-processed food, the NOVA scale, and THA's four-point stance on
> it.

## What it is

The help modal that explains ultra-processed food to a household. It sets out
what UPF means, introduces the NOVA classification scale, and lays out THA's
four-point stance on processing — the reasoning behind why the analyser judges a
food the way it does. It is the household-facing explanation sitting behind
THA's processing-first lens.
