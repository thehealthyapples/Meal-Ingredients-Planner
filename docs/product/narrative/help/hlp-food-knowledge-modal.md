# Food knowledge

> What a food is, why THA highlights it, what to know, and simpler alternatives —
> with an honest blank when THA has nothing.

## What it is

The food-knowledge modal a household opens on a food to learn about it: what the
food is, why THA has highlighted it, what is worth knowing, and any simpler
alternatives. In keeping with THA's discipline of citing what it knows, the modal
shows an honest blank where THA has nothing stored rather than filling the space
with a plausible line.
