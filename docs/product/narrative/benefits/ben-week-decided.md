# The week is decided, once

> A household plans the week in one place and turns it into a shopping list
> without re-deciding anything.

## What it is

The benefit of the weekly planner: a household decides what it is eating for the
week in a single place, and that decision flows straight into the shopping that
follows it — no re-deciding at the shop, no separate list to rebuild. The week is
settled once and then acted on.
