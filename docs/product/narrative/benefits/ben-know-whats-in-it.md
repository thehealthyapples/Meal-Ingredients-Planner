# You know what is actually in your food

> A household can scan any packaged product and see its additives and processing
> before they buy it.

## What it is

The benefit at the heart of THA's analyser: a household can scan or search a
packaged product and see what has actually been done to it — its additives, its
degree of processing, the things a front-of-pack label glosses over — before it
goes in the basket. It turns an opaque label into a plain answer.
