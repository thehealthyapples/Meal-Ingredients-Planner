# You eat a wider range of plants

> THA counts the different plants a household eats and suggests the next one to
> try.

## What it is

The benefit of THA's plant-diversity tracking: rather than counting calories, THA
counts the number of *different* plants a household has eaten and gently suggests
the next one to try. It turns variety into something visible and achievable, one
new plant at a time.
