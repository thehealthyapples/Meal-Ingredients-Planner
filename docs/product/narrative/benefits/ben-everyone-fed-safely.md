# Everyone in the house is accounted for

> Each person's diet and allergies are held once and respected everywhere THA
> suggests food.

## What it is

The benefit of building THA around the household rather than a single dieter:
each person's diet and allergies are recorded once, on their profile, and then
respected everywhere the product suggests food — the planner, the shopping, the
analyser. Nobody at the table has to be re-entered, and nobody is forgotten.
