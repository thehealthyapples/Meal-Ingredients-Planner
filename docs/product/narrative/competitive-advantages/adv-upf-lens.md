# Processing, not calories

> THA judges food by what has been done to it rather than by counting it, which
> almost no consumer food app does.

## What it is

THA's distinguishing lens: it judges a food by what has been *done* to it — its
degree of processing, its additives, where it sits on the ultra-processing
scale — rather than by counting calories or macros. This is the axis almost no
consumer food app measures on, and it is why THA's rating of a product can differ
sharply from a calorie-first verdict.
