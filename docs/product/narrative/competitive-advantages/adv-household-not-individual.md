# The unit is the household, not the dieter

> THA plans for everyone who eats at the table, including the people who did not
> install it.

## What it is

Almost every consumer food app models one person: the person holding the phone.
THA's unit is the household. It plans for everyone who eats at the table —
including the partner, the child, the flatmate who never opened the app — holding
each person's diet and allergies and respecting them in every suggestion. The
account is not the dieter; it is the kitchen.
