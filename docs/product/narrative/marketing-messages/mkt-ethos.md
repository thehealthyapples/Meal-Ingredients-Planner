# We don't believe in perfect diets

> We don't believe in perfect diets or constant tracking. Just better choices,
> made consistently.

## What it is

The ethos strip on the landing page — an italic line set apart from the value
blocks. It positions THA against calorie counting and rigid dieting: the product
does not ask a household to be perfect or to log everything, only to make better
choices consistently. It is the philosophy that explains why THA scores and
suggests rather than tracks and restricts.
