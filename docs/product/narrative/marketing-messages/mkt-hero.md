# Eat better. Without overthinking it.

> The headline claim on the landing page.

## What it is

This is the first line a household reads on THA's landing page — the `<h1>` on
the public home page, sitting under the brand line "Confidently better choices,
simply" and above the body "Scan food, understand what's inside, and make
confidently better choices - simply." It sets the whole promise of the product
in one sentence: that eating better does not have to mean tracking, counting, or
deliberating.
