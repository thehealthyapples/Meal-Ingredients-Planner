# Scan & Understand

> Instantly see what's in your food - no confusion.

## What it is

The first of the three value blocks on the landing page, carried by a scan-line
icon. It tells a household that THA will let them look at a packaged product and
immediately see what is in it — additives, processing, the things the label
makes hard to read — rather than leaving them to guess.
