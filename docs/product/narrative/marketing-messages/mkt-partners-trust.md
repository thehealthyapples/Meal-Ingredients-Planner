# Every partner has been reviewed

> Every partner featured here has been reviewed by the THA team.

## What it is

The trust line in the "Why we share these links" section of the partners page.
It assures a household that the small, curated selection of partners THA links to
has each been reviewed by the THA team for aligned values, genuine quality, and
services they would recommend to a close friend. The same section discloses that
some links may be affiliate links.
