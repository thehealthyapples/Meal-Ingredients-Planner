# Notification preferences

> The per-kind notification controls the Experience Architecture requires
> which do not exist today, in any form.

## What it is

Notification preferences are the per-kind controls the Experience Architecture
calls for — the settings that would let a household choose which notifications it
receives. They are recorded here as a hidden setting because the architecture
requires them, but they do not exist in the product today in any form.

## Evidence

No notification preference table, endpoint or surface exists anywhere in the
codebase. This entry documents a required setting that is absent, not one that is
present.
