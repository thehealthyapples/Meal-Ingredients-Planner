# Household settings

> Everything a household can change about how THA behaves — diet,
> allergies, budget, shopping, planner, nutrition targets and the Companion's
> voice.

## What it is

Household settings are the full set of choices a household can make about how THA
behaves for them. From one place they set their diet, allergies and exclusions,
their budget and shopping preferences, how the planner works, their nutrition
targets, and the voice the Companion speaks in. These are the household's own
levers, edited on the profile page and stored against the household.
