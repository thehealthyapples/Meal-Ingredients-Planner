# Partners

> A reviewed directory of wellness partners, reachable from the profile menu.

## What it is

A curated directory of wellness partners — yoga studios, nutritionists, sleep and
movement services and the like — reached from the profile menu. Each partner is
presented with a short description, its category and location, whether it works
locally or online, badges such as "THA Recommended", and a note on why The Healthy
Apples recommends it, with a link out to the partner. Households can search and
filter, and featured partners are surfaced first. The listings are held as a fixed,
reviewed set within the product.
