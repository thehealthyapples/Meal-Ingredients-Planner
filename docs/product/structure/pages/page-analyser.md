# Analyser

> Scan a barcode or search a packaged product to see its additives, its processing, and a better swap.

## What it is

The place to understand a packaged food. A household can search by name — ketchup,
cereal — or scan a barcode, and the Analyser shows what is inside: the additives it
carries (with the higher-risk ones called out), how heavily processed it is on the
NOVA scale, and an overall reading of its quality. Where it can, it points toward a
cleaner alternative — a better swap. Results can be filtered and compared, so a
household can weigh one product against another before buying.
