# Supermarkets

> Browse the supermarkets THA supports, by country — a complete page that nothing in the product links to.

## What it is

A finished directory of the supermarkets The Healthy Apples supports, grouped by
country with a filter for the United Kingdom, United States and Europe. When the
basket holds items, a household could export the whole basket to a store or search
for single items there. The screen is complete and works if reached directly, but
no button, link or menu anywhere in the product navigates to it — so in practice a
household never arrives here.
