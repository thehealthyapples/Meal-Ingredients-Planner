# Build a Meal

> A fast way to assemble a simple meal without the full recipe editor — built, working, and linked from nowhere.

## What it is

A lighter way to put a meal together: name it, then add its parts one at a time —
a basic item, a web recipe, one of your own meals, a fresh or frozen ingredient, or
a packaged product picked from the product picker — and save the result as a meal.
The screen is complete and functional, but nothing in the product links to it, so a
household reaches it only by knowing the address.
