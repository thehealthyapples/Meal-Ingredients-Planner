# Pantry (the Living Larder)

> The household's own larder, drawn as a real room — what the house normally
> keeps, arranged where it lives, read by looking.

## What it is

A front-on realistic elevation of the household larder, built from governed
production photographic assets: floating oak shelves holding real clamp-top
jars (each labelled at runtime with the household's own words), a spice rack
for small jars, a larder cupboard, cold-store drawer units for fridge and
freezer staples, a preparation table with the fruit on top, a household
cupboard and a pet corner. Every object is the household's real staple:
items with an approved production visual render as that artwork; everything
else appears as an honest chalk tag — never a guessed picture. Objects are
directly manipulable — drag (or use each item's menu) to add to Shopping
(which never removes the staple), mark running low, move between places, or
take out of the larder (reversible). Search adds a new staple, resolving
identity server-side. No quantities, fill levels or expiry are represented.
In Explore mode (`/pantry?mode=explore`), a knowledge hub and intelligence
panel share what THA knows about those ingredients. `/larder` redirects here.
