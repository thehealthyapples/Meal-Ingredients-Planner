# Pantry

> What is in the fridge, freezer and cupboard — and what THA knows about each of those ingredients.

## What it is

A record of what the household already has in. In Inventory mode, items are kept by
where they live — larder, fridge, freezer and fruit, plus household and pet-care
staples — and each can carry a "need" quantity so a low stock can be topped up
straight into the basket. In Explore mode, a knowledge hub and intelligence panel
share what THA knows about those ingredients. Knowing what is already at home helps
tailor meal suggestions and avoids buying things twice.
