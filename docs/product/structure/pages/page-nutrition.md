# Nutrition

> A household view of foods in the selected Planner week and nutrient knowledge linked to foods across available Planner history.

## What it is

The household's nutrition screen, headed "Nutrition", with two views. Foods
reports distinct ingredients and the existing plant and category counts for the
selected Planner week. Nutrients covers all available Planner history and shows
nutrient and benefit knowledge linked to foods; those coverage counts are not
amounts consumed. The room reports household and food facts. Interpretation and
recommendations belong to the Companion.
