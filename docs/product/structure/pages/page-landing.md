# Landing

> Explains to a visitor what The Healthy Apples is, and invites them to sign up or try it for twenty minutes.

## What it is

The first screen a signed-out visitor meets at the front door. It carries the
brand hero — "Eat better. Without overthinking it." — with the story of a
family navigating food choices through health challenges, three short value
blocks (Scan & Understand, Make Better Choices, Build Better Habits), an ethos
line, and a closing call to action. Two ways in sit side by side throughout:
create an account, or try the product free for twenty minutes with no signup.
The trial button quietly starts a demo session and drops the visitor straight
into the product.

Once a household is signed in, this address stops showing the marketing page and
sends them on to their Home screen instead.
