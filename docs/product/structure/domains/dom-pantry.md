# Pantry

> What the household already has at home, so planning and shopping do not
> buy it twice.

## What it is

The Pantry is a household's record of what it already keeps in. Its Inventory
mode organises food items across Larder, Fridge, Freezer and Fruit, alongside
household and pet-care items, and lets a household note how much of something it
still needs. An Explore mode sits beside it for looking further into the pantry.
Because THA knows what is at home, planning and shopping can lean on it rather
than adding things a household already has.
