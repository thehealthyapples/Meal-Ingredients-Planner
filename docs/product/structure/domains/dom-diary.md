# Diary

> A light, day-by-day record of what the household actually ate — without
> the burden of a tracking app.

## What it is

The Diary is a gentle daily log of what a household actually ate, kept without
the weighing and counting of a tracking app. Its Daily Log view steps through
the days, recording meals by slot — breakfast, lunch, dinner, snacks and drinks
— and can pull entries in from the Planner or import them from a CSV. A Progress
view charts how things are going over a week, month or year. Entries can be
noted quickly and left as light as the household wants.
