# Partners

> A reviewed directory of wellness partners and services that support a
> household beyond the basket.

## What it is

Partners is a curated directory of wellness services that support a household
beyond its food — yoga and movement teachers, nutritionists, mindfulness
coaches, sleep and family-wellness services, and more. Each entry carries a
short description, a category, whether it is local, online or both, and a note
on why THA recommends it. A household can filter by category and service type,
see featured partners first, and follow through to each partner's own site. A
transparency note explains that some links may be affiliate links and that
every partner has been reviewed by the THA team.

The partner directory is a fixed, curated list held in the app rather than
household-specific data.
