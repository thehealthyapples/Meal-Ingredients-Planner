# Plant diversity milestone notice

> **RETIRED** (PRESENCE2, 2026-07-20). A quiet acknowledgement each tenth
> different plant the household had eaten.

## What it was

A `low`-priority notice built on the household's all-time plant diversity count,
surfacing when that count was a multiple of ten. It was voiced through the
Personality Registry's celebration templates, so the Companion congratulated a
household for a round number: *"Nice progress — 40 different plants tried."*

## Why it is retired

**GEA13** forbids *"celebration effects for ordinary use"* and any mechanism
whose purpose is to make the household return. Eating a fortieth vegetable is
ordinary use. A count of it is information; the count wrapped in applause is a
reward, and THA does not reward households for living.

It was one of the last two scoring surfaces left in the platform after
`PRESENCE1` removed nineteen from eight rooms — and both survivors were the
Companion's own.

## What was NOT retired

The nutrition centre's `plantDiversity` count is untouched, and remains the
canonical all-time figure for the consumers that legitimately read it. Plant
diversity is a CONTESTED domain; nothing here recounted it, and nothing here
changed who owns it.

## What replaced it

[[ntf-household-story]] — an observation with no denominator, no threshold and no
verdict.
