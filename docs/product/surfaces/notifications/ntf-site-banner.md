# Site banner

> A message an admin can show every household at once, at the top of every page.

## What it is

A message an admin can show every household at once, at the top of every page. It
reads the banner setting from the server; when the banner is enabled and has
text, a green bar with that message appears across the top of the app. When the
banner is off or empty, nothing is shown.
