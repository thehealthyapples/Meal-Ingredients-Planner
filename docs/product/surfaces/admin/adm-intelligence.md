# Intelligence Benchmarks

> See how the Intelligence Platform scored against its benchmark suite.

## What it is

The capability, available to admins, to run and read the THA Companion Benchmark.
An admin runs a benchmark through the single Companion seam and reads back the
Overall Intelligence Score with its breakdowns, safety gates, top improvements
and regressions, failed questions, release readiness and score trend, and can
re-open or download any previous run's report. No score is computed in the
surface itself — every figure comes verbatim from a stored run result.
