# Observation Workbench

> Watch what the Intelligence Platform actually did, in production, turn by turn.

## What it is

The capability, available to admins, to watch the Intelligence Platform's live
runtime telemetry from the Observation Engine — capability health, intent
quality, context composition, companion outcomes, knowledge grounding, planner
activity, benchmark runs, and a raw diagnostics feed. It is a read-only view: it
computes nothing beyond display formatting and renders a missing figure as "—"
rather than a fabricated zero.
