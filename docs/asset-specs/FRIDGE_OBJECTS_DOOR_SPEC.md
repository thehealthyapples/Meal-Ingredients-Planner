# Fridge Living Objects — Door items (contact sheet)

## Canonical prompt

A product-photography contact sheet on a seamless, perfectly flat, uniform medium neutral-grey studio background (no gradient, no props, no surface, no cast shadows on the background). Six household refrigerator-door items are arranged in two tidy rows of three, each fully separated with generous empty grey space around it, no overlaps, each centred in its own cell: (1) a clear glass bottle of fresh whole milk with a metal foil cap, (2) a clear glass bottle of fresh orange juice, (3) a glass bottle of red tomato ketchup, (4) a yellow squeeze bottle of mustard, (5) a clear glass jar of mayonnaise with a lid, (6) a clear glass jar of green gherkin pickles with a lid. Each item photoreal, upright, softly and evenly lit from the front with neutral daylight, natural colour, sharp focus, life-like scale, floating clearly on the plain flat grey field like cut-out product shots.

## Negative constraints

No table, shelf, tray, box or packaging carton. No cast shadows on the background. No gradient or texture. No text you can read, no brand logos, no watermark. No overlapping items. No hands, no people. Nothing other than the six separated items on flat grey.

## Candidate strategy
Single text-to-image, 1536x1024, opaque; extracted by removing the flat grey background.
