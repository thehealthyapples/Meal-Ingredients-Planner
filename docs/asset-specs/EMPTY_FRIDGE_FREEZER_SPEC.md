# Fridge interior — physically derived from the SAME fridge-over-freezer appliance (edit)

The interior must be two views of the SAME appliance seen from Arrival: an
integrated oak french-door refrigerator ON TOP, a freezer DRAWER at the bottom.
Closing the doors from this interior must animate straight back to the Arrival
exterior with nothing changing. So: the two french doors open over the UPPER
refrigerator compartment only; a CLOSED oak freezer drawer occupies the lower third.

## Canonical prompt

A photorealistic interior view of the SAME integrated oak-panelled french-door
**fridge-freezer** built into warm honey-oak cabinetry on a pale stone floor. It is a
**fridge-over-freezer**: the **two oak french doors are open** revealing the
**REFRIGERATOR compartment in the UPPER TWO-THIRDS only** — three toughened-glass
shelves and, at the bottom of that refrigerator compartment, two clear salad crisper
drawers; white moulded door racks line both open oak doors. **Below the refrigerator,
occupying the LOWER THIRD, is a CLOSED oak-panelled FREEZER DRAWER with an antique
brass bar handle**, flush and continuous with the surrounding oak cabinetry — the same
freezer drawer seen closed on the exterior appliance. The refrigerator is COMPLETELY
EMPTY and clean (bare glass shelves, empty clear crisper drawers, empty door racks).
Warm interior fridge light in the upper compartment. Same oak integration, same warm
honey-oak cabinets, same stone floor, same camera angle square to the appliance, same
proportions and lighting. The refrigerator occupies only the upper section; the closed
freezer drawer is the lower section. Photoreal, warm, calm.

## Negative constraints

No stainless steel. No American branded fridge. NO full-height refrigerator — the
freezer drawer MUST occupy the lower third and be CLOSED. No food, jars, bottles or
produce inside. No open freezer. Do not change the surrounding oak cabinets, the stone
floor, the camera angle, framing, proportions or lighting. No text, no logos.

## Candidate strategy
Single edit from work-fridge-empty.png via images/edits. Reject if the refrigerator
still spans the full height or if there is no closed freezer drawer at the bottom.
