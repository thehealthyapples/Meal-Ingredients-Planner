# Living Home — Arrival Environment Plate · Generation Specification

Date: 2026-08-01
Status: Candidate generation only; not production approval (Home-Owner visual acceptance required — ASSET1 / LHDC1).

Purpose: the **Level-1 Arrival** Stage plate for the Living Larder (Spatial Blueprint §5). Front-on room; the household sees only destinations (fridge, freezer, shelves, cupboards, baskets); **no products**. Furniture is Stage (byte-constant); provisions are Living Objects added at runtime and must NOT appear here.

## Canonical prompt

Photorealistic architectural interior photograph of a warm English farmhouse larder, viewed front-on. Long-lens one-point perspective from a fixed tripod one step inside the doorway, camera at standing eye height, optical axis level — no tilt, no roll, no wide-angle distortion, straight verticals. The composition is calm and gently symmetrical: a fitted oak larder runs across a square back wall that fills most of the frame; a shallow band of flagged stone floor runs across the bottom; the top of the frame ends in a quiet band of chalky lime plaster above the highest shelf. No ceiling, beam, cornice or wall head is visible. Warm cream, honey, oat and natural-oak palette.

LEFT of frame: a pair of tall, floor-to-worktop integrated oak cabinet units with flush hand-made oak door fronts and simple aged-brass cup handles, reading unmistakably as fitted larder cabinetry — the fridge above, the freezer below. They must look like warm hand-built oak cupboards, never steel or white modern appliances. Doors closed and flush.

CENTRE of frame, closest to the camera and the clear focal point: open hand-built oak pantry shelving — three to four stout horizontal oak boards on simple brackets against the chalky lime-plaster back wall. The shelves are completely EMPTY: bare oak boards, no jars, tins, bottles, packets, food, plants or objects of any kind.

BELOW the shelving: a continuous run of oak lower cupboards and cup-handled drawers beneath a thick timber-and-stone worktop with a softly worn front edge.

RIGHT of frame: one tall window with a deep plastered masonry reveal set into the right return wall, bringing soft daylight into the room; on the worktop beneath and beside it stand two or three EMPTY woven-willow baskets — bare, holding no fruit, vegetables or produce.

Materials only: hand-troweled lime plaster in chalky off-white with visible trowel sweeps and soft density mottling; warm aged oak throughout with irregular per-plank grain, tone and gentle wear and a low satin sheen; a thick timber-and-stone worktop; a flagged stone floor. Lighting: one moderately high mid-morning sun outside to the room's right-forward quarter, entering only through the right-hand window and travelling inward and leftward; a soft sunlit patch lands coherently on the back wall and floor; every direct cast shadow runs down and to the left with consistent direction and softness; warm diffuse bounce from the lit floor and plaster keeps the shaded left side open without any second light source; gentle warm occlusion in every wall-to-wall and wall-to-floor junction. Natural contrast, clean shadow detail, no bloom, flare or vignette. Through the window, soft bright daylight gently blown out, with at most an unfocused suggestion of orchard green — no hard landscape, no glazing bars.

The room contains its FURNITURE ONLY and is entirely free of provisions and dressing: no jars, tins, bottles, packets, food, produce, fruit, vegetables, potted herbs, plants, flowers, textiles, labels, text, signage, artwork, UI or decoration of any kind. Editorial architectural photography, medium-format character, natural light, sharp throughout, realistic — not CGI, illustrated, painterly or stylised.

## Negative constraints

No jars, tins, bottles, packets, food, produce, fruit, vegetables, potted herbs, plants, flowers, sacks, crates, textiles, rugs, curtains, pictures, signage, labels, text, watermark, logo, UI. No stainless-steel, white or glass-fronted modern fridge or freezer, no visible appliance, no chrome, no control panel, no rubber door gasket. No people, animals. No wide-angle or fisheye distortion, leaning verticals, two-point perspective, tilted horizon, deep short-lens floor. No second sun, down-right cast shadow, mismatched shadow direction, uncaused glow, HDR, sepia, vignette, lens flare, painterly, illustration, clip-art or CGI. No visible ceiling, beam, cornice or wall head. No hard landscape, sky detail, or window glazing bars — soft bright daylight only.

## Candidate strategy

Generate candidates with this prompt. Permitted variance: plaster microtexture, oak grain and wear, exact sunlit-patch shapes, small exposure differences, and the precise counts (3–4 shelf boards, 2–3 baskets). Fixed and non-negotiable: front-on level one-point camera at eye height; the LEFT oak cold-cabinet pair (fridge over freezer, oak-panelled, never steel); the CENTRE open EMPTY oak shelving as focal point; the oak lower cupboards under a worktop BELOW; the RIGHT-hand window with daylight arriving from the right (shadows down-left); the complete absence of every provision and all dressing; warm oak + lime plaster materials at North Star editorial photographic quality.
