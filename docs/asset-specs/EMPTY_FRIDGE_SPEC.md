# Empty Fridge — Environment Plate (edit of work-fridge.png)

Phase 0. Edit the existing full fridge plate into an EMPTY Environment Plate that
passes the "five-seconds-earlier" test: the same fridge, moments before the
household placed any food into it. Furniture, camera, lighting and craftsmanship
must be preserved exactly; only the food is removed.

## Canonical prompt

A photorealistic interior photograph of a large open American-style stainless-steel
fridge-freezer, both french doors open, built into warm honey-oak kitchen cabinetry
on a pale stone-flag floor, lit by the fridge's own soft interior light with gentle
morning daylight from the right. The fridge is COMPLETELY EMPTY and freshly cleaned:
every toughened-glass shelf is bare, both clear plastic salad crisper drawers at the
bottom are empty and clean, and every white moulded door rack on both open doors is
empty. Nothing is inside — no food, no jars, no bottles, no cartons, no tubs, no
produce, no eggs, no packaging — only the clean empty interior, the bare glass
shelves, the empty drawers and the empty white door racks. Keep exactly the same
fridge, the same open doors, hinges and handles, the same interior shelf layout and
crisper drawers, the same surrounding oak cabinets above and beside it, the same
stone floor, and the same camera angle, framing, depth of field and lighting — as if
this is the identical photograph taken five seconds earlier, before the household
filled the fridge. Same room, same furniture, same materials, same craftsmanship,
photoreal, natural, calm.

## Negative constraints

No food of any kind. No milk, juice, bottles, cartons or jugs. No jars, tins or
containers. No cheese, butter, eggs, yoghurt or dairy. No vegetables, salad, fruit or
berries. No jam, ketchup, condiments, pickles or sauces. No leftovers or prepared
meals. No packaging, labels or text. No clutter on the shelves or in the drawers or
door racks. Do not change the fridge, the cabinets, the room, the floor, the camera
angle, the framing or the lighting. Do not introduce a different fridge or a
different kitchen. No people, no hands.

## Candidate strategy

Single edit candidate from work-fridge.png via images/edits (no mask), preserving
composition. Reject and note if the room, fridge, camera or lighting change, or if
any food remains.
