# Fridge Living Objects — Salad drawer (contact sheet for extraction)

Phase 0. Six salad Living Objects, evenly spaced on a flat mid-grey background so
each can be cleanly cut out to a transparent PNG and placed independently in the
empty fridge crisper drawer.

## Canonical prompt

A product-photography contact sheet on a seamless, perfectly flat, uniform medium
neutral-grey studio background (no gradient, no props, no surface, no shadows on the
background). Six fresh salad vegetables are arranged in two tidy rows of three, each
one fully separated from the others with generous empty grey space all around it, no
overlaps, each centred in its own cell: (1) a small cluster of ripe red tomatoes on
the vine, (2) a whole fresh cucumber, (3) a glossy red bell pepper, (4) a small bunch
of red radishes with leaves, (5) a whole round lettuce, (6) a small bunch of spring
onions. Each vegetable is crisp, fresh, photoreal, evenly and softly lit from the
front with neutral daylight-balanced light, natural colour, sharp focus, life-like
scale, floating clearly on the plain grey field like cut-out product shots.

## Negative constraints

No table, no board, no plate, no bowl, no basket, no packaging, no bags. No cast
shadows on the background. No gradient or textured background. No text, no labels, no
watermark. No overlapping items. No hands, no people. Nothing other than the six
separated vegetables on flat grey.

## Candidate strategy

Single text-to-image candidate, 1536x1024, opaque. Objects extracted by removing the
flat grey background to transparency, then cropped per grid cell.
