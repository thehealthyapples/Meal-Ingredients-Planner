# Fridge Living Objects — Shelf & fruit (contact sheet)

## Canonical prompt

A product-photography contact sheet on a seamless, perfectly flat, uniform medium neutral-grey studio background (no gradient, no props, no surface, no cast shadows on the background). Six fridge items are arranged in two tidy rows of three, each fully separated with generous empty grey space around it, no overlaps, each centred in its own cell: (1) a block of butter half-wrapped in gold foil on a small white dish, (2) a wedge of hard yellow cheese, (3) a small round tub of plain yoghurt with a lid, (4) a clear glass food-storage container with a clip lid holding cooked leftovers, (5) a clear plastic punnet of mixed berries (strawberries, blueberries, raspberries), (6) a bunch of red grapes. Each item photoreal, softly and evenly lit from the front with neutral daylight, natural colour, sharp focus, life-like scale, floating clearly on the plain flat grey field like cut-out product shots.

## Negative constraints

No table, board, shelf or surface. No cast shadows on the background. No gradient or texture. No readable text, no logos, no watermark. No overlapping items. No hands, no people. Nothing other than the six separated items on flat grey.

## Candidate strategy
Single text-to-image, 1536x1024, opaque; extracted by removing the flat grey background.
