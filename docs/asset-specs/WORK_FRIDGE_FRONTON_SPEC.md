# Living Home — American Fridge Working Position (canonical, front-on, text-to-image)
## Canonical prompt
Photorealistic front-on interior photograph of a large American-style double-door refrigerator, BOTH doors fully open, viewed straight on from directly in front at standing eye height, the open refrigerator filling the frame. It is built into warm hand-made oak farmhouse cabinetry with chalky lime-plaster surrounds and soft natural daylight, so it belongs in a handcrafted English country kitchen — never a cold commercial showroom. The interior is clean and lit by warm LED, with tempered glass shelves, door shelving on both doors, a lower bottle shelf, a dairy shelf and clear salad crisper drawers at the bottom. It is organised like a real family refrigerator by storage location, not supermarket merchandising: top shelf — covered leftovers and prepared-meal dishes; middle glass shelves — tubs of Greek and natural yoghurt, wrapped cheddar and mozzarella, a block of butter; a lower bottle shelf — glass bottles of milk and jugs of juice; both door racks — jars of mayonnaise, ketchup, mustard, jam and pickles; the clear salad drawers — lettuce, spinach, cucumber and peppers; a small berry area — strawberries, blueberries and raspberries; a bowl of eggs. Homely and real, comfortably filled, not packed, not symmetrical, no printed brand labels. Editorial architectural photography, warm interior light with soft daylight, sharp, realistic — not CGI, illustrated or stylised.
## Negative constraints
No side view, no angled or partly-open door — both doors fully open, seen straight on. No supermarket shelving or merchandising, no brand packaging, printed labels, text or logos. No cold stainless-steel commercial styling — warm handcrafted. No clutter, no perfect symmetry. No people, animals, CGI, illustration or stylisation. Both doors and the full interior visible; nothing cropped awkwardly.
## Candidate strategy
Text-to-image, 1536x1024, high. Fixed: straight-on front view; both doors fully open; warm oak/plaster handcrafted surround with soft daylight; interior organised by storage location (leftovers top, dairy middle, bottles lower, condiments in the doors, salad drawers, berry area, eggs); homely not merchandised; North Star editorial quality.
