# Living Home — Bread Working Position (dedicated camera, text-to-image)
## Canonical prompt
Photorealistic close front-on photograph at standing eye height, the household now standing directly at the bread store of a warm English farmhouse larder. On a stone worktop: a handcrafted lidded wooden bread crock, and a wooden board beside it holding a couple of rustic sourdough loaves, a few bread rolls and a bagel, with a folded linen cloth. Warm aged oak, chalky lime plaster and stone frame the view — a repositioned view within the same handcrafted room, not the wide room and not a zoom. Homely and real, comfortably arranged, no packaging or labels. Editorial photography, natural light, sharp, realistic — not CGI or stylised.
## Negative constraints
No wide full-room shot, no zoom/crop of a room image. No plastic wrappers, packaging, labels, text or logos. No clutter, no people, CGI or illustration.
## Candidate strategy
Text-to-image 1536x1024 high. Fixed: close eye-height view facing a wooden bread crock and a board of rustic loaves/rolls/bagel; warm oak/plaster/stone; repositioned in the same room; homely; North Star quality.
