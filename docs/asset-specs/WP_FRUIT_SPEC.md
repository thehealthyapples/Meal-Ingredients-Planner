# Living Home — Fruit Working Position (dedicated camera, text-to-image)
## Canonical prompt
Photorealistic close front-on photograph at standing eye height, the household now standing directly at the fruit baskets by the window of a warm English farmhouse larder. Two or three woven willow baskets sit on a stone worktop, generously and naturally filled with ready-to-eat fruit — red and green apples, bananas, pears, satsumas and oranges — catching soft daylight from the window just beyond. Warm aged oak, chalky lime plaster and a stone worktop frame the view; a repositioned view within the same handcrafted room, not the wide room and not a zoom. Homely, real, comfortably full, no packaging or labels. Editorial photography, natural light, sharp, realistic — not CGI or stylised.
## Negative constraints
No wide full-room shot, no crop/zoom of a room image. No vegetables, no onions/potatoes here (fruit only). No packaging, labels, text, logos, plastic. No clutter, no people, CGI or illustration.
## Candidate strategy
Text-to-image 1536x1024 high. Fixed: close eye-height view facing willow baskets of ready-to-eat fruit by the window; warm oak/plaster/stone; repositioned in the same room; homely; North Star quality.
