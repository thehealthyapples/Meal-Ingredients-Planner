# Living Home — Root Vegetable Rack Working Position (dedicated camera, text-to-image)
## Canonical prompt
Photorealistic close front-on photograph at standing eye height, the household now standing directly at the root vegetable rack in a warm English farmhouse larder. A handcrafted three-tier black powder-coated steel wire rack stands on a flagged stone floor, naturally filled with loose root vegetables kept cool and dry — potatoes, sweet potatoes, brown onions, whole garlic bulbs and shallots. Warm aged oak cabinetry and chalky lime plaster frame the view in soft daylight — a repositioned view within the same handcrafted room, not the wide room and not a zoom. Rustic, real, comfortably filled, no bags, packaging or labels. Editorial photography, natural light, sharp, realistic — not CGI or stylised.
## Negative constraints
No wide full-room shot, no zoom/crop of a room image. No plastic bags, packaging, labels, text or logos. No shiny chrome — matt black steel wire. No clutter, no people, CGI or illustration.
## Candidate strategy
Text-to-image 1536x1024 high. Fixed: close eye-height view facing a handcrafted black-steel wire rack of loose potatoes, sweet potatoes, onions, garlic, shallots; warm oak/plaster; repositioned in the same room; homely; North Star quality.
