## Canonical prompt

Photorealistic architectural interior photograph of the open oak shelving wall of a warm English farmhouse larder, seen close and straight-on, as if the household is now standing right in front of the shelves. Long-lens one-point perspective, camera at standing eye height, optical axis level — straight verticals, no tilt, no roll, no wide-angle distortion. The shelving fills most of the frame at a comfortable working distance where a single storage jar would sit large and legible in the hand. Warm cream, honey, oat and natural-oak palette.

The wall shows three to four stout horizontal oak shelf boards on simple hand-made brackets, spanning the frame, set against a chalky off-white lime-plaster back wall. Each board is a thick, aged warm-oak plank with irregular grain, softly worn front edges and a low satin sheen. The shelves are completely EMPTY — bare oak boards, no jars, tins, bottles, packets, food, plants, labels or objects of any kind. A shallow suggestion of the timber worktop may sit at the very bottom edge; no cupboards, no window frame, no cabinetry fronts fill the frame — the shelving wall is the whole subject.

Materials only: hand-troweled lime plaster in chalky off-white with visible trowel sweeps and soft mottling; aged warm oak with irregular per-plank tone, grain and gentle wear. Lighting: one moderately high mid-morning sun from the room's right-forward quarter, arriving from the right and travelling leftward; soft sunlit modulation across the plaster and oak; every direct cast shadow — from each board and bracket — runs down and to the left, consistent and soft; warm diffuse bounce keeps the shaded areas open with no second light source; gentle warm occlusion where boards meet the wall. Natural contrast, clean shadow detail, no bloom, flare or vignette.

The shelves and wall are entirely free of provisions and dressing: no jars, tins, bottles, packets, food, produce, potted herbs, plants, textiles, labels, text, signage, artwork, UI or decoration. Editorial architectural photography, medium-format character, natural light, sharp throughout, realistic — not CGI, illustrated, painterly or stylised.

## Negative constraints

No jars, tins, bottles, packets, food, produce, fruit, potted herbs, plants, flowers, baskets, textiles, labels, text, watermark, logo, UI, signage. No cupboard doors, no fridge, no freezer, no window frame or glazing filling the frame. No people, animals. No wide-angle or fisheye distortion, leaning verticals, two-point perspective, tilted horizon. No second sun, down-right cast shadow, mismatched shadow direction, uncaused glow, HDR, sepia, vignette, lens flare, painterly, illustration, clip-art or CGI. No visible ceiling, beam or wall head.

## Candidate strategy

Generate candidates with this prompt. Permitted variance: plaster microtexture, oak grain and wear, exact light-patch shapes, small exposure differences, and the precise number of shelf boards (3–4). Fixed and non-negotiable: close, front-on, level, eye-height one-point camera at a jar-legible working distance; three to four EMPTY horizontal oak shelves as the whole subject; right-hand daylight (shadows down-left); the complete absence of every provision and all dressing; warm oak + lime plaster at North Star editorial photographic quality.
