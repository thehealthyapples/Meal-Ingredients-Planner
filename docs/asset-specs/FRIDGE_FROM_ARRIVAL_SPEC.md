# Fridge Working Position — derived FROM the Canonical Pantry photograph (Camera Truth)

Base image: arrival.png — the canonical room itself. The camera walks to the fridge
and the doors open. NOTHING else about the room may change. This is the §8.1.2 rule:
a camera records reality; it never invents it.

## Canonical prompt

Take this exact pantry photograph and move the camera three steps forward and to the
left, so it now stands directly in front of the tall integrated fridge-freezer on the
left wall, framed square-on, the appliance filling most of the frame. It is the SAME
appliance already visible in this photograph — keep its exact dark mottled
antique-bronze oak finish with its aged patina, its twin long antique-brass door
handles, the matching transom panel above the doors (part of the same unit), the
single deep freezer drawer below with its horizontal brass bar handle, and its exact
real proportions (refrigerator behind the french doors in the upper section, the deep
freezer drawer the lower section). Show the two french doors now standing OPEN,
revealing a clean, completely EMPTY white refrigerator interior — three toughened
glass shelves, two clear crisper drawers at the bottom of the compartment, and white
moulded storage racks on the inside of each open door. The freezer drawer below
remains CLOSED. Everything else stays exactly as photographed: the same soft plaster
walls, the same stone-flag floor, and at the right edge of the frame the same oak
open shelving and stone worktop of this pantry continuing naturally out of frame.
Same warm morning light from the right, same craftsmanship, same room. It must read
as the same photograph taken from a new position with the fridge doors opened.

## Negative constraints

NO curtains anywhere — this room has no curtains. No new cupboard above the fridge —
only the appliance's own matching transom panel. No stainless steel, no chrome, no
light honey-oak appliance. No food inside. Do not change the walls, floor, shelving,
lighting or any other part of the room. No people, no text, no logos.

## Candidate strategy
Single edit candidate with arrival.png as the base (the room is the ground truth).
Reject if: curtains appear, the finish lightens, the freezer drawer shrinks, or the
right edge of frame does not show this pantry's own shelving/worktop.
