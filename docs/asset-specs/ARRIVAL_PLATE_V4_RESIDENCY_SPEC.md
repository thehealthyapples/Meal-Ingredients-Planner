## Canonical prompt

Refine this exact photoreal English farmhouse-larder interior. Keep completely unchanged: the camera, composition, perspective and light direction; the integrated oak fridge-freezer; the three open oak shelves and brackets; the stone worktop; the oak lower cupboards, drawers and the four-legged wooden stool; the window; the willow baskets; the ceramic bowl; the vintage scales; the folded linen; the wooden chopping board; the flagged stone floor. Do not move, resize or restyle any of them.

Add only these few permanent objects, which quietly show a household that grows, ferments and cooks real food. Place each naturally, with room around it — never crowded, never staged:

1. **Growing microgreens** — one shallow earthenware or wooden seed tray of dense, thriving fresh green microgreens (pea shoots / cress), set on the **windowsill on the right where the daylight falls**, so it reads as genuinely alive and cared for. Vivid living green, not dried or decorative.

2. **A handcrafted kombucha ferment** — one large traditional glass demijohn or ceramic fermentation crock of amber tea with a visible SCOBO/culture, its mouth covered by a simple cloth tied with string, standing **on the worktop**, clear of the other objects. Handmade and homely — never a commercial branded bottle or shop product.

3. **One well-used favourite cookbook** — a single cloth- or leather-bound cookbook, gently worn, standing or leaning **at one end of the lowest shelf or against the wall on the worktop**, suggesting someone cooks here often.

Nothing else is added. No clutter, no filling of empty space, no extra props. Everything must look owned and used, not arranged for a photograph. Editorial architectural photography, natural light, sharp, realistic — not CGI, illustrated or stylised. Everything not listed above stays exactly as it is.

## Negative constraints

No additional objects beyond the three above. No commercial or branded packaging, no shop bottles, no labels/text/logos. No change to the fridge-freezer, shelves, worktop, cupboards, stool, window, baskets, bowl, scales, linen, chopping board, floor, camera, composition or light. No clutter, no crowding, no over-styling. No food on the open shelves. No people, animals, CGI, illustration or stylisation. Microgreens must look alive and fresh (not wilted, dried or fake); the kombucha must look handmade (not commercial).
