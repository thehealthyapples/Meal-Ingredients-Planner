# Living Home — Store Cupboard Working Position (dedicated camera, text-to-image)
## Canonical prompt
Photorealistic close front-on photograph at standing eye height, the household now standing directly in front of an open oak store cupboard in a warm English farmhouse larder — BOTH cupboard doors open, the interior filling most of the frame. Inside, natural oak shelves are naturally stocked with a real household's tinned and long-life goods: plain tins of chopped tomatoes, beans, soups, tuna and coconut milk stacked in rows, with a few glass storage jars. Warm aged oak, chalky lime plaster and soft daylight — a repositioned view within the same handcrafted room, not the wide room and not a zoom. Homely, comfortably stocked, no printed brand labels (plain tins). Editorial photography, natural light, sharp, realistic — not CGI or stylised.
## Negative constraints
No wide full-room shot, no zoom/crop of a room image. No brand packaging, printed labels, text or logos on the tins. No clutter, no people, CGI or illustration. Both doors open, interior clearly visible.
## Candidate strategy
Text-to-image 1536x1024 high. Fixed: close eye-height front view of an open oak cupboard (both doors open) stocked with plain tins & jars; warm oak/plaster; repositioned in the same room; homely; North Star quality.
