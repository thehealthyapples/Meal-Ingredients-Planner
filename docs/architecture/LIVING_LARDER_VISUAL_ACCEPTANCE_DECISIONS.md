## D-018 — The current /pantry application composition is rejected

**Finding (Home Owner):** the live `/pantry` still feels like a conventional webpage with a fancy room on it. Each principal Healthy Apples area must instead feel like entering a different physical room.

**Rejection evidence:** a large orchard masthead, page title, editorial heading, search/add toolbar and rounded content container preceding the room image; below it, repeated labelled shelf strips, cards, ellipsis menus and vertical document scrolling reproducing an inventory/dashboard hierarchy. Furniture is not experienced as one continuous navigable room.

**Governing outcome — the room-first contract (binds any future Larder room, including the preserved committed implementation):**

1. The architectural scene is the dominant viewport and the first information layer.
2. No masthead, search or content card may sit above the room.
3. No repeated category strips may form a long document beneath it.
4. Furniture and provisions belong inside one continuous scene.
5. Mobile shows a narrower view of that same scene — never a list substitution.

**Downstream gate:** `v4`–`v8`, pantry-data binding, PR and deployment remain blocked until the room-first contract is met **and** Home Owner visual approval is obtained. A clean technical pass is not evidence of visual acceptance (D-018).
