# Principles and Product Constitution

## Core engineering principles

- One entity: every concept exists once.
- One owner: every fact has one authoritative owner.
- One source of truth: consumers never become owners.
- Extend before creating: improve existing capabilities before adding new ones.
- Honest gaps: unknown is preferable to fabricated certainty.

## Product constitution

The Healthy Apples is a household companion. Technology should become quieter as it improves. Hospitality comes before productivity. Intelligence removes effort rather than creating dependence. The product should strengthen households, not maximise engagement.

Prefer presence over attention, trust earned through restraint, and progress that feels lived rather than measured. The best interface knows when to disappear. Intentional forgetting can be an act of care. When the reading is ambiguous, prefer the option that adds less.

## Visual/product implication

Living Home and Living Larder should feel like a real, cared-for home rather than a dashboard. Preserve the established environmental, spatial, layered-rendering, asset, and Living Object architecture. Do not replace a mature visual system with a parallel simplified implementation without explicit approval.
